# Critic and writer diagnostic gains have not established a whole-story quality benefit

Local ID: **CW-014**. Status: **OPEN QUALIFICATION GAP**; the full workflow canary remains **ON HOLD**.

## Problem

Valid reports, repaired obligations, small diffs and high model craft scores do not establish a better reading experience. The current diagnostic candidates need evidence that their judgments and edits help stories rather than merely improve their own test scores.

## Existing evidence and limits

Full-story human evaluations already exist. The completed v33 three-repeat assessment recorded 34 reviewed pairs: seven Cloud wins, ten baseline wins and seventeen ties. Preference acceptance failed in all three repeats. That is historical evidence, not a current v61 comparison or an uncompleted review task.

The October human review also finished: nine overall ties and 45 dimension ties across one closely related scene family. The reader found a promising story foundation and no meaningful preference between these variants. That does not establish statistical equivalence or literary harm from extra edits.

The latest reviewer diagnostic used twelve pairs from three scene families, one seed and fixed original/revision presentation. Only one pair used a saved actual writer revision; the others were constructed or no-op controls. Gemma/C1's 7/12 usable assessments are promising diagnostic evidence, not a calibrated editorial gate. Older local prose-score swings likewise lack sufficient calibration to human experience.

## Proposed evaluation direction

First resolve the local interface and scoring ambiguities. Later use fresh, meaningfully different stories and a comparable baseline under the accepted rubric. Humans should judge overall feel/emotion, flow, character and story-breaking concerns. Machines should prefill counts, version identity, recorded repair outcomes and technical checks. Do not require people to search eighteen nearly identical versions again.

## Closure criteria

- [ ] Define the intended quality claim and representative evaluation before selecting examples or running calls.
- [ ] Obtain comparable whole-story evidence, with independent human comparison, order controls, ties and uncertainty preserved.
- [ ] Separate technical completion, critic accuracy, preservation and literary preference in acceptance decisions.
- [ ] Demonstrate the applicable accepted quality/preference criteria before claiming qualification.

This is evaluation work, not authorization to run a canary, start Step 20, or add a mandatory production human checkpoint. No new experiment or budget is frozen by this draft.
