## Interpretation and next focused test

The added packet improved exact preservation in both paired seeds while the mixed
controls remained repairable. This supports the handoff hypothesis: explicitly
carrying the latest review's positive evidence can help the writer distinguish
existing fulfillment from work still needing repair. It does not prove an internal
model mechanism or isolate the status, excerpt, assessment and instruction components.

**Keep v59 and the diagnostic projection fixed.** The next focused test should
transfer this comparison to a different scene with valid indirect fulfillment and
an independent defect, plus a genuinely unmet-requirement control. Preserve the
packet and critic rather than tailoring instructions to this successful passage.
Before production integration, further qualification must also cover a cited met
passage that itself needs correction for another defect; positive evidence must
not become an untouchable span.

These diagnostic packets come from saved validated reviews bound to exact drafts.
A production handoff would need its own durable source/projection design and checks;
this experiment does not add such a path or authorize another cloud run. It is not
a full-story, human-preference or broad prose-quality acceptance result.
