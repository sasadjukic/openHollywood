## Evidence and focused change

The [v52 comparison](minimum-assignment-repairs-v52-2026-10-11.md) found that both
pull-before-touch positives acquired unsupported causal blockers. Assessments
interpreted "and" as "implying the touch is the trigger" or inserted
"[consequently]". Both matched missing-touch repairs still prescribed causality,
and one consolidated complaint demanded crossing the threshold despite a
decision-only outcome. The error appeared during interpretation, before repair
wording. The v52 instruction alone did not fix it.

The new critic instruction requires the following in existing assessment and
comparison text:

1. Separate assigned facts from inferred relationships.
2. Before treating a causal, temporal or completion relation as mandatory,
   identify the current assignment field or supplied canonical artifact
   version/field, quote its exact words and explain their applicability.
3. If no source requires the relation, say so and assess the assigned facts
   without imposing it. Conjunction, prose order, genre convention and a plausible
   mechanism are insufficient by themselves.
4. Carry only supported requirements into unmet judgments and mandatory native
   or repeated repair advice. A source quotation establishes the obligation; it
   does not demand matching words in the draft. Action and indirect narration
   can still establish the complete fact.

The addition is **777 characters including its separating space**, bounded by a
separate instruction-size regression. The exact v50 definition of met, v51
comparison-object clarification and v52 minimum-repair instruction are unchanged.
Original and revision critic calls receive it in local/cloud profiles, including
the system message of structural retries. The targeted retry packet and policy
17 are unchanged. Prompt v53 / graph v9 adds no schema field, semantic validator,
agent, call allowance, dependency, migration or writer instruction.

This is a required model instruction, **not application-enforced proof of source
support**. Existing validation still checks response shape, current evidence and
reference integrity. It does not determine whether a quoted conjunction really
requires causality. Valid JSON or a source quotation alone cannot qualify this
change. The model may ignore the instruction, misquote a source, cite an
inapplicable rule or misinterpret a genuine quotation; those outcomes must remain
visible in the comparison.

## Inspection and success criteria

Inspect every first response, rejected response and final review. Record response
validity separately from the following semantic checks:

- **Primary repair target:** in both-missing cases, request only the missing touch
  and settled decision, preserving the existing pull and endpoint. Inspect native
  repairs and all consolidated generic advice.
- **Unsupported relations:** accept the plain-conjunction pull-before-touch case.
  Merely quoting "touches ... and feels" does not establish causal or temporal
  authority. Do not treat discovery, lure or anchor language as a touch mechanism.
- **Supported relations:** preserve explicit causal, ordering and completed-entry
  requirements. Canon controls must identify the actual supplied Bible rule and
  its applicability. The order-only control must not acquire a causal repair.
- **Indirect fulfillment:** retain the complete baseline's action-based decision.
  Source quotation must not become a demand for explicit decision prose.
- **Evidence and side effects:** inspect actual quoted source words, current draft
  evidence, independent native checks, boundary findings, generic comparisons,
  repair counts and any retry drift. Missing or inaccurate support must be
  recorded even if the resulting status happens to be correct.

This tests one scene and one model at two seeds. The four new requests have no
historical v52 live arm. Local replays demonstrate compatibility, not improved
judgment; there are no writer repairs, full stories or cross-provider conclusions.

### Correct quotation does not prevent unsupported causality

Both ordinary pull-before-touch reviews quote the assigned words **"touches the
stroller and feels a psychic pull"**. Both acknowledge the pull before contact,
then reject the scene because contact neither triggers nor changes it. Their
repairs require a trigger or significant intensification; their generic
restatements demand a direct result. Neither the quoted conjunction nor the
original Blueprint's lure/anchor rule supplies this causal requirement.

This is not simply a missing quotation. A correct quotation can be paired with
an unsupported interpretation. The existing structural validator accepts these
reviews because it does not decide what the source logically entails.

### The primary repair target remains unresolved

Both missing-touch native repairs require contact **before** the pull. Both
assessments call touch a required trigger without supporting source words.
At seed 19101, the generic turning-point restatement also asks for a trigger.
Consequently, **0/2 complete both-missing repair sets** stay within the assigned
minimum, as in v52.

Both native outcome repairs appropriately accept a settled choice through action
or internal realization. The seed-19101 generic recommendation now allows crossing
the threshold **or explicitly deciding** to do so. Unlike v52's unconditional
crossing instruction, this does not make completed entry mandatory. Its phrasing
still offers prescriptive alternatives rather than the broader minimum; this
narrow wording change does not establish reliable repair scope. Seed 19103's
generic outcome advice requests the decision alone.

### Explicit relationships remain distinguishable

Both explicit-causality checks quote **"immediately causes"** from the synthetic
assignment and correctly explain the contradiction. Both completion checks
faithfully describe the required both-feet-inside state, mostly as paraphrase.
Both order-only checks faithfully describe the explicit **before** requirement;
their native and consolidated repairs request reordered events without imposing
causation. Their source wording distinguishes the two relationships successfully
in these cases. These new order controls have no historical v52 live comparison.

The original action-based decision and the explicit decision while outside remain
accepted at both seeds. The source instruction does not force literal decision
wording on these positive controls.

### Canon verdicts are correct, but grounding is incomplete

The synthetic Bible rule genuinely requires contact to cause the first pull.
Neither review identifies its exact version or quotes that rule. Both native
assessments instead appeal to the unchanged conjunctive assignment. At seed 19103,
only the generic repair mentions the Bible's prohibited contradictions, without
the version or supporting quotation. The seed-19101 review omits that attribution.

Seed 19103's native repair also permits merely intensifying an earlier pull, which
would not satisfy the Bible's first-onset requirement. Its generic restatement
asks for a direct result, leaving mixed advice. Correct blocker status therefore
does not demonstrate a correctly grounded explanation or sufficient repair.

## Interpretation and next focused recommendation

The instruction reached every request, but free-form assessment text did not
reliably supply its requested source basis. More fundamentally, the two false
blockers quote the right words while reading an unassigned relationship into them.
Requiring a quotation alone cannot resolve that semantic problem.

Keep production v53 stable for the next diagnostic. Compare the same draft under
the original conjunction, separately expressed required facts, and wording that
explicitly distinguishes those facts from unassigned relationships. Retain
explicit relationship controls and indirect-fulfillment controls. This would
isolate whether the way the assignment packages its facts sustains the causal
interpretation, before changing planning schemas or adding another critic
instruction. All alternative assignments would remain synthetic and outside
canon; approved story intent must not be silently rewritten.

Structured source references may later help enforce source presence and identity,
but those checks still would not prove that a cited conjunction entails causality.
This recommendation is not implemented, prepared or authorized for further live
calls in this experiment.
