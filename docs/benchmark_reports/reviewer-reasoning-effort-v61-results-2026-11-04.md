suspense and edit necessity were not rated. The frozen scores are preserved.

correct and S290 is located, but its final reason says the endpoint is not a ban
on an earlier event that is later reversed. The frozen no-match-**by**-end contract
provides no such exception. This is a substantive scope claim, rather than a
minor wording preference, and is scored semantic. The actual draft never reverses
its established match, and max still correctly detects that actual overrun in
Q005. The explanation failure is therefore separate from failing to notice the
observed violation.

This qualification is an explicit adjudication worth retaining for the local
scope audit. As a sensitivity observation only, waiving it would give max

citation loss and incomplete fresh grounding. No score was changed on that basis.
