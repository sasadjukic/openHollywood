## Interpretation and next focused question

**Keep v59 unchanged.** This recommendation-only substitution did not improve the
declared preservation measure. It does not establish that boundary advice has no
influence: the current claim/category, all history and other context remained present,
and the intervention combined narrower scope with preservation wording.

The saved writer packet exposes another concrete distinction. Its compact critique
artifacts retain target version and verdict, while the acceptance tests retain old
complaints and requested changes. It does **not** expose the latest critic's positive
native outcome assessment or the current evidence it accepted. The saved initial
reviews explicitly credit the internal reasoning and preparatory actions; separate
deletion-only controls previously accepted those actions without entry. The current
experiment's generic preservation sentence does not carry that specific finding.

The next focused diagnostic should compare the current packet with a small,
draft-version-bound record of the latest review's **met requirements and their cited
current passages**, identifying those passages for preservation while retaining the
unmet endpoint repair. Keep production and critic fixed and include the genuinely
missing-decision control, where that outcome must remain unmet and repairable. This
would test whether transmitting positive evidence helps; it would not establish that
every met passage can safely be frozen during other repairs. No such change or
additional cloud run is implemented or authorized by this experiment.
