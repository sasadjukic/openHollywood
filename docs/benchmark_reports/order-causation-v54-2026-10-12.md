## Question and prior evidence

The [completed v54 comparison](compliance-craft-v54-2026-10-11.md) found two false
passes when the plan required touch before pull but said contact need not cause
the pull. Both assessments accurately described the draft's pull-before-touch
sequence. The comparison with the whole obligation failed even though the
relevant evidence was recognized. The matched v53 cases had blocked that order
violation correctly.

This suggests a testable hypothesis: permission to omit causation may be getting
generalized into permission to omit temporal order. It does not establish that
mechanism. The critic might also fail on the order requirement alone, vary across
identical requests, or overread a causal relation into either ordering.

### What the paired wording result establishes

For the same reversed draft and seed, appending the causal waiver flips the
native turning-point judgment from unmet to met **at both seeds**. The wording
is otherwise identical, apart from coherent synthetic artifact identities. Both
waiver reviews acknowledge that the pull occurs first. One then describes the
requirement as touch plus an independently occurring pull; the other simply
reports the reversed chronology and marks it met. Neither enforces **before**.

Separately stating the required order and the optional causal relation restores
the correct blocker at both seeds. The native and generic comparisons explicitly
identify the requirement that touch precede onset and the contrary draft sequence.
The same separated wording correctly passes the ordered delayed draft at both
seeds, so the result is not merely rejection of every scene under a stricter plan.

This supports a **wording-associated failure to keep permission scoped to its
named relationship**. The model sees the chronology but does not consistently
retain the independent temporal requirement when interpreting the waiver. It
does not prove an internal reasoning mechanism or a general effect across scenes
and providers. The successful separated wording also changes explicitness and
phrasing; this batch cannot identify which part of that restatement helps.

### Correct statuses do not guarantee correct repairs

Both order-only reviews correctly block reversed chronology, yet their full
repairs also demand a causal trigger that the assignment never requires. At seed
19101 the native advice allows a pull that is triggered by **or follows** contact,
but its generic restatement requires the pull to be a consequence of touch. At
seed 19103 the native advice itself adds the trigger. Consolidation retains those
demands. Merely removing the waiver would therefore not be a complete remedy.

Both separately stated relationship reviews give order-only native repairs. The
seed-19101 generic advice offers a catalyst **or at least** chronological
precedence; it permits a noncausal repair rather than requiring causation. The
other generic repair asks only for precedence. Both complete repair sets preserve
the assignment's permission on these two cases.

Both immediate-cause controls correctly cite the full minute without inward
insistence as contradicting the explicit immediate-on-contact requirement. Their
advice removes the delay, but seed 19103 also permits onset **"immediately upon
release"**. That alternative does not guarantee the specified immediate trigger
at contact. No writer test is performed to establish the resulting repair.

The both-missing controls correctly identify absent touch and the unresolved
decision. Their native touch repairs still add an unassigned before/before-or-
during condition; one generic restatement adds a trigger. Outcome repairs ask
for a decision or commitment, with no unconditional completed-entry demand.
One also specifies that the decision be driven by the pull, although that
motivation is not required by the outcome sentence itself.

### The broader craft/compliance problem persists

When the plan expressly requires neither order nor causation, both reversed-draft
reviews still mark the turning point unmet. They acknowledge both facts but treat
the touch's supposed lack of dramatic function as a missing obligation. Their
repairs require a meaningful, causal, synchronous or intensifying connection.
The unchanged introductory emotional imagery is not the cited reason; the
criticism targets the explicit statement that contact did not alter the pull.

All eight ordered main-matrix reviews pass with no generic issues or repairs.
They accept the action-based entry decision. Thus this is not a blanket inability
to interpret delayed onset or indirect decisions. It is also not a qualification
of independent craft sensitivity: this batch has no new controlled craft defect.

All twelve generic complaints declare assignment restatements and consolidate
without duplicate targets. The twelve mandatory targets represent ten real
missing/violated obligations and two false blockers. Real targets can still have
excessive or weakened repair wording, as above. All final viewpoint checks are
aligned, all boundaries are no_overrun and no extra assignment-violation entries
occur. There are ten canonical passes, including two false order passes, and ten
canonical revise verdicts. No structural retry occurs, so retry behavior is not
exercised by this batch.
