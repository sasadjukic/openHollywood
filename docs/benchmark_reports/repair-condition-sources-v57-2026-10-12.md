## What is inspectable and what is validated

Every model-written assignment repair must have one entry in the required
`assignment_repair_conditions` array, including native unmet checks, other
assignment violations, viewpoint repairs and linked generic restatements at any
severity. Each lists its mandatory conditions separately. Every condition has:

1. The exact condition text that will form part of the repair advice.
2. One to three source references, each with an exact quoted passage.
3. An assessment explaining why the cited source requires that condition here.

Condition strings joined with one space must equal the full native or generic
repair recommendation. A valid restatement link cannot conceal extra advice
outside those records. The application also checks exact route coverage, current
source membership, verbatim contiguous quotations and bounded object/text shape.
It never rewrites an excessive condition into a smaller one automatically.

The source catalog resolves to exact artifact versions and original JSON Pointer
paths. It preserves original array indexes after context filtering, distinguishes
identical text at different locations, and excludes draft/reviewer allegations.
It contains current assignment fields and selected visible Blueprint/Story Bible
content. Catalog inclusion does not make a character preference or broad dramatic
intention mandatory; applicable source scope remains a semantic judgment.

Independent craft criticism retains its existing evidence and comparison route.
Met checks and independent craft do not require assignment-condition entries.
The boundary repair already authored by application policy is unchanged; a model
restatement adding to it must be covered. Immutable historical repair advice is
not silently re-authored or retroactively certified by this new field.

The validated records remain inspectable in invocation audit schema 5, including
resolved version/path identities and redacted source quotes. They do not become
story canon. Canonical recommendation text and the existing shared writer/critic
repair-test contract remain unchanged. There is no database/public API schema
change or migration. Prompt version advances to **57**, graph remains **9**,
and critic structural retry policy advances to **18** without increasing retries.

**Traceability is not semantic support.** A model can cite a genuine order clause
and incorrectly claim it requires causation. It can also group multiple demands
in one condition. Exact coverage exposes the complete advice; it does not prove
atomic decomposition, source applicability, entailment or creative merit. A local
test explicitly accepts such a source-valid but semantically unsupported repair,
preventing structural success from being mistaken for the remedy.

See [ADR 0034](../adr/0034-traceable-assignment-repair-conditions.md).

### Source records expose a second, conflicting advice channel

All **20 repair-bearing attempts** fail at the same first error: the condition
strings do not reconstruct the native recommendation exactly. The model writes
requirements as condition statements, then independently phrases repair advice.
The retry's explicit equality instruction does not correct this behavior.

Post-run inspection of unmodified raw responses finds **24 repair records, 41
conditions and 41 citations**. Every supplied citation resolves to the current
Scene Plan and quotes an exact passage from turning_point or outcome. The listed
conditions do not demand more than those cited assignments in this sample, but
**all 24 native repair texts differ** from their records. Two retries also add a
linked generic issue with no corresponding `issue:0` condition record (seed 19101,
causal waiver and immediate-cause control); the earlier native mismatch is the
reported first error. Those two route omissions are additional inspection findings.

The equality check catches faithful paraphrases as well as substantive additions.
For example, a condition says touch must precede pull while separate advice says
to reorder the sequence. Both can express the same obligation but fail the wire
contract. Other differences conceal the very problem under investigation:

- Both order-only cases, on both attempts, still demand that contact **trigger**
  the pull. Their condition records cite and describe order only. The source does
  not require causation.
- Both missing-touch cases, on both attempts, still demand touch **before or
  during** the pull, despite the assignment explicitly imposing no order. Their
  condition records require only the assigned facts.
- The seed-19101 missing-decision retry additionally demands moving both boots
  across the sill into the interior. Its source and condition require a decision,
  not completed entry. The first attempt did not add that demand: repair advice
  can drift while the unmet status remains stable.

Thus four of the ten last repair-bearing raw responses retain excessive advice,
including five native repair routes. This is not an improvement claim over v55's
five excessive real-defect reviews: **none of these v57 repairs is accepted**.
The other six final native repairs stay within the assigned relationships on
inspection. Immediate-cause final advice preserves causation and onset at contact;
the causal-waiver generic alternative permits the temporal precursor without
requiring cause. Both generic additions still lack required condition records.

No independent or optional craft issue is emitted. Summaries and scores still
sometimes call temporal order a causal setup or trigger, including where the
assignment waives causation. This vocabulary is diagnostic context, not an extra
accepted repair. The ten pass reviews receive 5/5 on all three dimensions;
these ratings are not independent evidence of prose quality.

The structured source route is inspectable, but a nonempty validated audit was
**not** achieved live. Nonempty accepted audit persistence and linked-generic
coverage are established only by local tests; failed live records are available
in bounded failure evidence and the private response archive. No applicable-canon
source was selected live. Semantic support and provider transfer remain unqualified.

### Recommendation

Keep v55 on main. The new exact-coverage safeguard correctly prevents unaccounted
advice from being accepted, but asking the model to maintain identical repair text
in two places is unusable in this sample. Do not loosen coverage to accept this
run's outputs or silently delete the uncited parts of existing responses.

Next, test a contract in which the structured conditions are the **single authored
source of mandatory assignment advice** and the application renders the canonical
recommendation from them. Cover native and linked generic contributions; reject
conflicting extra advice instead of choosing one version silently. Preserve the
existing independent-craft route and v55 meaning of met. First test deterministic
rendering, coverage and consolidation locally, then run a bounded matched live
comparison. Rendering would guarantee textual coverage, not that cited sources
semantically authorize each condition. This is a recommendation, not implemented
in v57, and no additional model calls are authorized or made here.
