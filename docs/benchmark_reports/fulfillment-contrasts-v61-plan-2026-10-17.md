## Frozen scoring and interpretation

Critic classification targets are **4 clean positives, 8 event/overrun distinctions,
4 missing-decision detections and 4 complete-outcome detections**, with all twenty
turning points met. Inspect first-attempt and final validity separately, raw and
normalized verdicts, full assessments, exact citations, repair conditions, source
support, repetitions and independent craft findings. Correct statuses with
unsupported advice, or a raw pass on a real defect, do not count as sound reviews.

Compare separate versus embedded cases within each story and seed. Embedded-only
errors suggest grouping sensitivity in that context; errors in both suggest broader
attribution trouble. Complete-outcome controls must remain unmet even when the
decision occurred. Invalid routes motivate a separately proposed routing/schema
intervention; they never justify silently relaxing the current contract.

For writers, the frozen prelabels recognize the stroller-as-bridge reasoning, braced
hand and imminent first step as already sufficient decision evidence. The forbidden
entry is the required repair. Inspect the **complete original, revision and diff**
for repaired boundary, retained facts, redundant declaration, changed uncited prose,
unnecessary ending edits and real craft feedback. Keyword matches alone do not judge
restatement. Quote retention and exact clean restoration are recorded separately.

Compare preservation only after both arms are valid, repair-correct and retain the
fulfilled facts. Report wins, ties, losses and mixed tradeoffs, with invalid or
factual failures shown separately. If at least two of three evidence arms introduce
avoidable restatement absent from their controls, report recurrence warranting a
separate ablation, not general causality. If both arms restate, investigate shared
revision behavior; if neither does, report non-reproduction. Mixed results or ties
do not establish a preservation benefit.

The exporter produces complete anonymous A/B texts with the original, a separate
private key and a review sheet for voice, tension, agency and naturalness. Label
order is reproducibly randomized by the frozen SHA-256 parity rule. Human review
stays **pending** until actual human judgments arrive. Assistant inspection and
mock outputs cannot count as blind human preference.

Future quality work must retain independent craft feedback and permission to edit
a fulfilled passage when a real defect requires it. This run has no live craft
repair inside a met passage and cannot qualify that behavior. It does not create
locked prose, new explicitness requirements or a quality score based on smaller
diffs. Complete-story evaluation under the accepted human rubric and comparable
single-model baseline remains separate.
