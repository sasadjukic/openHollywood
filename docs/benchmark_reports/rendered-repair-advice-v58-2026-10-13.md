### Remaining failures and semantic limits

Both missing-facts first reviews correctly identify the absent touch and absent
decision and supply the two native condition records. They also repeat both
findings in generic issues, but omit `issue:0` and `issue:1` condition records.
The review fails exact route coverage instead of creating an incomplete critique.

- Seed 19101's retry retains correct native judgments but returns a linked
  outcome restatement with non-null free-text advice and no condition record.
  It fails the single-authority rule.
- Seed 19103's retry retains correct native judgments, drops the two repeated
  issues and introduces a purported independent pacing issue. Its comparison
  lists no current findings despite both native blockers remaining. It fails
  the existing independent-craft comparison rule.

Both raw verdicts remain reject; no assignment-status or raw-verdict drift occurs.
Generic issue content does change. The invalid first condition contract prevents
those hard findings from qualifying for classification retention, so the second
review is not constrained by preserved generic classifications. No failed review
produces accepted canonical output or a computed writer target.

The application's strict boundary is working, but the model still sometimes
reasons inaccurately. Both accepted order-only delayed drafts mention causation
in their check assessments even though the assignment requires only order. Both
reversed order-only reviews use "catalyst or" preceding-event language in support
assessments. These four cases do not add a causal repair requirement, but they
prevent a claim that the critic now distinguishes those relationships reliably
in all its reasoning. Source traceability is still not semantic certification.
All twenty-two prose-quality scores are 5; this experiment does not validate
those scores or broader story quality.

### Local diagnosis and next focused change

Explicit local copies of the two failed first reviews validate after adding the
missing generic condition records by copying each issue's referenced native
record. Nothing else is changed: judgments, classifications, comparisons and
null advice fields remain intact. This isolates the first failures to redundant
per-route coverage. The resulting recommendations repeat the same advice after
the application's restatement label. These copies are not model results and are
not counted among the eighteen accepted live reviews.

The next focused experiment should give each consolidated finding **one condition
set**, with declared restatements referencing that set instead of requiring
another authored copy. Additional mandatory conditions must live in the same
authoritative set; independent craft must retain its own comparison. Do not
silently backfill current responses or discard advice under the existing
contract. The proposal needs a deliberate wire change and tests, including
boundary/POV links, genuinely new conditions and invalid references. Keep v55 on
main while that route remains unreliable.
