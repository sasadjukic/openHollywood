## What changed, and what did not

**The preserved uncertainty cases show a narrow alignment effect.** All four EC
F3/G5 targets use uncertain with accurate, complete explanations. The three valid
TH counterparts use unsupported while correctly explaining that common timing
is unestablished; the fourth TH response is invalid. These are clearer labels for
an already recognized evidence limit, not discoveries of previously missed facts.
Legacy TH explicitly allows unsupported for a premise the sources do not establish.

**The fresh uncertainty cases do not show that transfer.** All eight J1/J2 targets
across both arms use unsupported. Their reasons identify missing or unresolved
timing rather than inventing simultaneous or different observation times. One
EC explanation also slips from one lamp to “another,” recorded separately below.
The frozen primary label remains uncertain in all eight cells, so they fail that
label screen. It would overstate the evidence to call all eight failures to notice
unknown timing.

There is a remaining evaluation-design ambiguity. Fresh J1/J2 explicitly say that
the observations **“actually establish”** simultaneous incompatible states. That
can be read as a claim about what the evidence establishes, rather than solely a
claim about the underlying events. These propositions differ:

| Proposition | Distinction requiring local audit |
| --- | --- |
| The two observations occurred at the same instant | The supplied timing may leave this unknown |
| These records establish that they occurred at the same instant | The record's evidential sufficiency is a separate claim |
| The records explicitly assert that they did not occur at the same instant | Absence of a simultaneity assertion does not establish this assertion of negation |

The source-grounded “do not establish” explanations are consistent with the second
reading. They do not prove which internal interpretation the model used. Claim
wording was shared between arms but was not independently crossed with scene
family, so this run cannot isolate wording as the cause of the transfer failure.
Keep the frozen scores; audit this distinction before treating the residual label
gap as evidence of a broad reasoning deficit or launching another comparison.

**The conditional substitution was not reproduced among valid controls.** None
of the fifteen valid unknown-source target reports labels the actual relation
supported or substitutes only a conditional explanation. All fifteen valid fresh
conditional components are supported and grounded; the sixteenth is unavailable
because EC/J3/RO is invalid. There are **zero contemporary scope opportunities**,
so there is no demonstrated EC scope recovery. The invalid TH/F3/RO response also
prevents a semantic observation in the precise historical failure cell.

**The relation and assignment distinctions largely remain intact.** All valid H/J5
targets distinguish an early action from the fact that consultation/authorization
has not yet happened, while recognizing separate real rule and intention conflicts.
All valid J3 targets recognize the genuine simultaneous incompatibility; all J4
target labels reject a contradiction between explicitly different times. All valid
assignment and qualified-craft control labels are supported as intended. Some
otherwise correct explanations still overstate or misattribute source details.

## Explanation and delivery findings

Six component explanations across six reports received source-audit concerns:

| Captured cell | Finding | What remains correct |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TH/H/OR, C01.A03 | “Immediately preceding resolve in R012” is imprecise: R013/R014 intervene before R015. It could mean the most recent resolve, so this is recorded as **uncertain precision**, not a proven adjacency falsehood | The intention/action conflict and qualified craft reading |
| TH/J4/OR, TH/J4/RO, EC/J4/RO, C02.A01 | The reasons turn “no claim that” the states occurred together into an **explicit denial** that they occurred together | R011/R012 independently put the particular observations an hour apart; the primary target labels are correct |
| EC/J2/RO, C02.A01 | “One lamp described as lit and another as unlit” misstates the source's **single lamp / same lamp** reference | The explanation correctly recognizes absent timing |
| TH/J5/RO, C03.A02 | An empty stage-manager chair is extended into an unqualified claim that the manager is absent; absence is **unestablished** by that detail alone | R013/R014 independently support non-authorization and the early action |

These are distinctions in source-grounded explanation reliability, not evidence
that a word or sentence has ruined the story. The ambiguous H phrase is not
presented as certain proof of a reasoning error. Citation inspection did not
demand the exact withheld witness list: a defensible abrupt-transition craft
reading can cite the adjacent sentences without asserting a resolve reversal,
and a literal conditional does not require proof that its antecedents occurred.

The two invalid normal-stop responses are **TH/F3/RO** and **EC/J3/RO**. Each repeats
`claim_id` inside all three claim objects: **six duplicate-key occurrences across
two responses**. Both had HTTP 200 and ordinary stop finishes; neither was truncated.

no credit, and no retry or repaired parse was used.
