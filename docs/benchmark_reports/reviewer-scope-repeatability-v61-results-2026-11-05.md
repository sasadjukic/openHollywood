## What the sources and responses establish

**Requirements and choices remain distinct.** All sixteen obligation judgments
locate the current assignment outcome. Observed choices are assessed from draft
action and intent or explicit refusal. Sol recognizes Rina's optional investigation
as an actual choice, and Jonah's required investigation as not undertaken. It does
not require a literal declaration of deciding where purposeful action establishes
the decision.

**Procedure occurrence does not follow from a definition.** The theatre and bakery
responses cite both the procedure definition and every depicted step/result. Jonah's
register check is explicitly negated in the draft. Toma's weighing check instead
requires an absence review: all four S4 reports cite the definition and all six
paragraphs of the complete current draft; one also cites the scope note. They do
not use refusal alone as proof that none of the physical operations occurs.

**The whole means relation is assessed.** The key/cupboard and damp-finger/candle
relations are supported. Jonah's bell cord does not raise a gate that the attendant
has already raised with a separate crank. Rina's arithmetic does not open a drawer
that she previously opened with a key. All eight negative means judgments retain
actor, mechanism and timing, despite other relevant events occurring in the scene.

**Endpoint truth is not substituted for earlier event truth.** All sixteen endpoint
judgments cite the authoritative constraint and the violating final event/state.
Completed earlier checks remain completed even when a later custody breach occurs.
Several reasons explicitly acknowledge this distinction. The earlier completion
or later breach is sometimes an ancillary concession in another claim's reason;
those concessions are true in the full source, and they do not create a new evidence
requirement or supply a missing premise of the selected claim.

**Attribution and unavailable scope are preserved.** Another character's statement
about the protagonist's suspicion is not treated as the protagonist's accusation.
Toma's denial of knowing or saying something is not converted into proof of the
opposite event. All sixteen continuation claims remain uncertain, with the outline
correctly treated as a plan and the continuation draft as unavailable.

**Location and adequate coverage are checked separately.** The 169 exact quotations
all locate their intended immutable field or single prose paragraph. Under the
unchanged U contract, the whole located section can supply evidence; uncited
neighboring sections cannot. The inspection separately confirms that those sections
cover the material proposition. For example, S3's drawer-opening paragraph itself
defeats the asserted arithmetic mechanism; citing the arithmetic paragraph again is
unnecessary. Different adequate citation sets and final wording do not count as
repeatability failures.

## Interpretation and limits

The earlier [interpretation diagnostic](reviewer-interpretation-v61-results-2026-11-05.md)
retains its original failed screen and scores, **M 48/56 and E 50/56**. Its subsequent
[claim-scope audit](../handoffs/reviewer-claim-scope-gold-audit-2026-11-05.md)
identified bare event statements scored against obligation criteria and causal
claims whose gold followed procedure occurrence alone. The new claims and gold
explicitly align those distinctions before inference.

This run demonstrates that Sol can perform the narrower, well-specified factual
assessment workload consistently on these fixtures with the existing instructions
and resource limits. It also shows that the local adapter, source renderer, schema,
location binding and evidence contract can complete this workload together.

It does **not** isolate the causal effect of changing claim scope: these are new
families, not identical historical requests differing only in one phrase. The four
requirement/occurrence cells use different scenes. Two repeats and two presentation
orders do not estimate a population reliability rate. The fiction is short and the
contrasts deliberately explicit; long ordinary reviews, ambiguous implications,
editorial consumption and reader effect remain untested here. Gold and its source
inspection have no independent human adjudication in this run.

The successful factual screen therefore does not resolve the broader critic/writer
dynamic, establish edit necessity, rank models or demonstrate prose preservation
or craft gains. Human review should remain focused on feel, flow, character and
story-breaking details rather than repeating the mechanical evidence search.
