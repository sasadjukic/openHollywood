## How strongly does this read as an AI-generated story?

`The Root Ritual` reads strongly as an AI-generated story. It starts with contrastive framing.

`Dawn broke over the village not with light, but with a bruised, purple haze that clung to the eaves of the decaying cottages.`

Our writer repeats many of the well-documented AI tics, with contrastive framing appearing more than 20 times.

## Opinion

>Note! The following opinion is for The Root Ritual as it was outlined in Open Hollywood's pre-production and later created in production after blind human approval.

`The Root Ritual` is a decent horror story. Our main characters are fine and the pacing of the story is okay. The main problem with this story is that we never see and feel the threat of the carnivorous flora that surrounds the villagers. We are told of the threat.

It is in people's nature to fight until the end, while our villagers keep accepting the sacrifice to appease the carnivorous flora. When dealing with groups, we cannot assume that all members of a group will act the same. In stories, they shouldn't. It makes stories less interesting.

In `The Root Ritual`, we never see a villager attempting to break out of the area. Even if they don't succeed, we must see an attempt that ends horribly, so we understand the decision of those who are willing to submit.

I do like that Mara attempts to save Kael in scene 4. I like that she fights against "The Unseen" in scene 6. But to fully depict the danger, we should've seen something tragic in scene 1 that would've set the tone and the stakes for the rest of the story.

I feel that if our writer had tightened the prose and the dialogue, with a few changes to bring the danger the villagers face closer to the reader, `The Root Ritual` could've been a much better story.
