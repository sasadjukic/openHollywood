## Opinion

>Note! The following opinion is for The Chronophage as it was outlined in Open Hollywood's pre-production and later created in production after blind human approval.

`The Chronophage` has an interesting premise and a decent main character. If you're able to cut through all the LLM patterns of our writer, you'll find that `The Chronophage` has elements of a good story. 

I do feel our main character, Lara, could've been developed a bit more. What turned her into a predator? What's her background? Can this journey be turned into a sort of journey of self-revelation?

Lara's fights could've been more interesting as well. What if she encounters a fragment she doesn't want to swallow? Or perhaps she swallows a fragment that causes her to vomit? Overall, it feels like she cruises to the end without major problems.

The ending of the story is fine, but the way to the end could've been a much more interesting journey.
