## How strongly does this read as an AI-generated story?

`The Unblinking Bloom` reads strongly as an AI-generated story. The story starts with contrastive framing:

`Dawn arrived not with a song, but with a suffocating stillness.`

Our writer relies heavily on contrastive framing, which appears over 20 times.

## Opinion

>Note! The following opinion is for The Unblinking Bloom as it was outlined in Open Hollywood's pre-production and later created in production after blind human approval. This is not my attempt to rewrite the story from scratch.

`The Unblinking Bloom` is a decent story. Our protagonist, Elara, has a very interesting ability, and she's facing a life-altering choice. That is all good, but I do believe our writer could've established a stronger connection between Elara and the rest of the village.

When drought comes and food becomes scarce, there's strong uncertainty within groups. This leads to divisions within groups over how to deal with the danger of starvation. Instead of telling us, as our writer did in scene 2, `The children's tongues are swelling. The elders are drifting into delirium.`, our writer could've shown a delirious or hungry person whom Elara knows. Perhaps Elara witnesses this person die or go crazy. That way, we can experience this suffering instead of just being told about it. Experience always has more impact on the human mind than words.

A terrifying experience would also carry a lot of weight in scenes 4 and 5, when the roots and Elder Kael urge Elara to sacrifice herself. At some point during this debate, we could've heard a child screaming or seen something else bad happen. This, too, could've helped convince Elara to submit to her sacrifice.

`The Unblinking Bloom` is similar in some details to `The Boundary Line`, another story written by Open Hollywood.
