## Limits and implications for production

This is an identified assistant review of a fixed, fully read inventory. The
annotations are not a blind human review, automatic semantic classifier or an
independent adjudication of every original assertion. The scripts replay those
annotations only against the exact pinned inventory. Original labels, scores,
quoted text and saved validation outcomes remain unchanged.

The practical result is that the read-only component can represent ordinary
critic explanations without erasing their craft concerns. It can enforce identity
and expose authored qualifications; it cannot decide a good interpretation, prove
truth or determine whether an edit is necessary. The factual candidates still
need whole-field gold and adequate evidence, including any embedded quotations,
source attribution, temporal qualifiers and implied relations.

Source-scope declarations are also not model citation budgets. Here the local
registry can describe complete paragraph coverage. That does not establish that
the existing response citation limit can express adequate evidence for every
whole-scene absence claim. This must be checked before freezing a live workload.

Story quality remains a separate question. Keep feel, flow, characterization and
story-breaking details available for human quality review, supported by prepared
mechanical evidence. Do not use this audit to turn editorial concerns into factual
errors or to introduce another routine human checkpoint into production.
