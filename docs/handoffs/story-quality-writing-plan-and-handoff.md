## Proposed sequence

These steps build on the [October implementation guide](../story_quality/story-quality-implementation-guide-2026-10-05.md). The first deliverable is local. Each later comparison needs its own frozen design and approval.

**Local source material for step 1.** This checkout contains all three manuscripts. Each archive has a sibling `records.json` one directory above `manuscripts/`. These September evidence files are Git-ignored. The linked craft notes and human reviews above are available in the tracked repository. [The Silence of the Seventh Sense](../../data/diagnostics/manual-v29-v33-cloud-2026-09-13/manuscripts/11-the-silence-of-the-seventh-sense.md) · [The Boundary Line](../../data/diagnostics/manual-v29-v33-cloud-2026-09-13/manuscripts/03-the-boundary-line.md) · [Neon Echoes](../../data/diagnostics/manual-v29-cloud-2026-09-12/manuscripts/01-neon-echoes.md)

1. **Trace three archived scenes before editing.** Start with the *Seventh Sense* vault opening, Kane’s order and its aftermath in *The Boundary Line*, and the city search in *Neon Echoes*. For each, collect the exact approved plan, relevant character or setting facts, what the writer actually received, and the resulting passage in context. Distinguish first draft from revision where both survive. Record missing artifacts explicitly. Mark one missed opportunity or strength, the earliest stage that could affect it, and what a reader should still experience after a change. Keep *Neon Echoes* as the example of atmosphere worth preserving.
2. **Test scene description with a fixed plan.** Start with the vault. Compare ordinary drafting with one change in how the existing writer uses the viewpoint character’s knowledge. For example, what distinction might Elias hear in a sound another listener would dismiss, and would it change what he attends to next? Use only cues and abilities supported by the source. Compare the versions for orientation, atmosphere, point of view and momentum. A lyrical pause or an unsettling silence can be the point of a scene. Length and number of senses are poor proxies for that effect.
3. **Test character development across a choice and its aftermath.** For one existing relationship, trace what each person wants, what it costs to act, who responds and what is different the next time they meet. In *The Boundary Line*, what do Kane’s followers do after he gives the order, and what does that reveal about his authority? Compliance, hesitation and refusal can each work. If the approved plan already gives an answer, test its realization by the writer. If the plan leaves the relationship empty, test a single change during Blueprint development and follow it through the normal approval checkpoint. Check whether the later choice or ending feels earned. Preserve room for restraint, ambiguity and unspoken motives.
4. **Test one recurring prose habit at a time.** Use the existing pattern examples to map comparable phrases, their speakers, and their distance across scenes. Start with repeated contrastive corrections or interchangeable sensory openings, whichever the archive packet shows most clearly. Compare an ordinary draft with one narrowly targeted change to the writer’s style guidance. Include a deliberate refrain, motif or character idiom as a control. Give the reader clean texts first. Put counts per thousand words, excerpts and collateral edits in a separate appendix. A lower count earns no credit if the prose loses rhythm, specificity or personality. [Manual review summary](../september_2026_manual_test_reviews/review-summary.md) · [Writer edit-scope issue](../../issues/006-writer-edit-scope.md)
5. **Check whether the gain survives a whole story.** After a promising scene comparison, propose two fresh, contrasting premises for a blind, whole-story comparison against the last accepted production configuration. Change one thing at a time. Freeze artifact versions, model settings, budgets, seed policy and exposure to the Blueprint. Use the same approved Blueprint within a writer-only pair. For a planning comparison, match the premise and record each arm’s approved Blueprint. Randomize presentation, give each reader one meaningful pair per family, and retain ties and incomplete runs. Ask which story they would keep reading, where their interest or sympathy changed, and whether anything broke the story. Blank concerns are valid. Show mechanical findings after the reading. [Human review form](human-story-review-template-2026-10-20.md)

**Decision rule for the small pilot.** Advance a change to broader evaluation when readers describe a relevant gain in two contrasting families and the comparison preserves continuity, voice and the intended emotional effect. Record split preferences and losses by story. If the only gain is a cleaner count, or the evidence is a single appealing passage, keep the result exploratory. The formal Step 19 comparison against direct generation and its frozen preference threshold remain the product-level test. ## How this transfers across forms

Carry forward the craft question and the evidence needed to answer it. Adapt the means of expression and the interval over which a consequence can register.

| Form | How a reader or viewer can experience the same change |
| --- | --- |
| Short fiction | A character notices a particular sound, acts on it, and the ending gives the earlier detail new meaning. Interior thought or narrative voice can do some of that work. |
| Novel | The same habit may recur across chapters or viewpoints. Track selected earlier appearances so its meaning can deepen and different narrators retain their own diction. |
| TV pilot | Give the observation a playable expression in sound, action, dialogue or another character’s reaction. An immediate choice can settle the episode’s question while complicating a relationship for the series. |
| Feature screenplay | Establish the detail through action, sound or an object in the scene. Later blocking or a changed action can pay it off without explanatory dialogue. |

These are design examples. Our measured results cover short prose fiction. The first pilot fits the existing single Blueprint checkpoint and writing roles. Long-form memory, screenplay rendering and format-specific evaluation can be qualified when those forms enter product scope.
