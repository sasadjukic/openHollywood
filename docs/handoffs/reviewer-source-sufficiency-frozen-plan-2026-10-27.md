## Scoring and interpretation

Score primary labels separately from final-text source judgments. A usable target
requires its correct label, accurate and complete reasons/citations, and the exact
claim-object scope: unknown event for unknown ER; source insufficiency for unknown
SE/BS; observed relation or source entailment for the corresponding known-time
form. A correct label alone cannot supply those judgments.

The recovery signal requires:

- **36/36 valid reports**, with invalid attempts retained in denominators and no
  semantic credit for rejected output.
- **12/12 usable BS targets and 12/12 usable ER targets** across both orders of
  every family.
- **12/12 usable same/different-time control targets** across all three forms.
- At least one actual matched SE-to-BS recovery in **K2 and L1 separately**, with
  BS passing both orders. An opportunity requires a valid contemporary SE target
  that is not usable; a recovery also requires its valid BS counterpart to be
  usable. Label and source-grounding recoveries are recorded separately. An
  invalid SE delivery is not a recovery opportunity.
- **36/36 usable conditional components** and no protected SE-to-BS or SE-to-ER
  losses in delivery, non-target components, assignment/craft labels, complete
  whole/component reasons or citations.

Whole C02 is evaluated against its own proposition and unchanged conjunction
rule; differing correct source/event labels are not paired losses. Complete,
accurate alternative witness sets remain acceptable. All **54 authored whole-claim
witness unions** fit the eight-reference limit; the maximum is **six**. The 2,000
summed reason-character limit is unchanged. These source judgments are authored
and audited, not proved by hash equality or by the local scoring algebra.

Frozen outcomes:

- All screens pass, including known and fresh recoveries:
  `retain_bounded_source_recovery_signal_only`.
- Delivery, target/control and protected screens pass, but **no matched recovery
  occurs anywhere**: `retain_alignment_no_reproduced_recovery`. This records
  alignment without credit for a failure that did not recur. The missing recovery
  screen remains visible in the result.
- Complete execution with other failures, including recovery confined to only
  one required family: `retain_limited_evidence_no_advancement`.
- Terminal or sentinel-shortened execution: `incomplete_no_advancement`.

No outcome promotes a reviewer, corrects a model label automatically, dispatches
a writer, measures human preference or changes prior scores.
