# Proposed event-relation versus source-entailment diagnostic

Date: 2026-10-27. **PROPOSAL ONLY — at most 24 calls. Not prepared, frozen or
approved for inference.**

The [local claim-object audit](reviewer-claim-object-audit-2026-10-27.md) identifies
a materially different hypothesis: the residual unsupported labels may respond
to an assertion that the records **establish** a relation, rather than express
failure to recognize that the underlying timing is unknown. The next test should
vary that claim form while holding the source and reviewer instructions fixed.

## Question and isolated comparison

Does the same high-thinking EC reviewer distinguish **a narrated observation
relation** from **the claim that a particular source establishes that relation**?

| Arm | Model-visible claim form |
| --- | --- |
| SE, source-entailment control | The supplied source establishes that the particular opposite-state observations concern the same referent at the same instant |
| ER, event-relation contrast | The particular opposite-state observations concern the same referent at the same instant |

Both arms use the **unchanged EC system definitions and component contract**,
`deepseek-v4.1-flash:cloud`, `think: "high"`, and the same complete source packet.
Only the target factual claim's object/form changes, together with its corresponding
whole-claim text, component proposition and necessary claim/version hashes.
Within a family, referent, observations, timing language and logical content must
otherwise match. Preserve the separate conditional component and all assignment
and craft controls. Do not add a system hint, source-scope card, new output enum,
thinking-effort arm, attribution treatment or writer call.

For the known J1/J2 SE controls, preserve the **four historical EC wire requests**
exactly, subject to a matching execution baseline. Their explicit “actually
establish” wording is retained. ER removes the source-establishment predicate and
the associated metalinguistic qualification without adding the expected answer.
The requested relation concerns the two observations; it is not an assertion that
an impossible physical state is true in the real world.

**The proposed SE gold is prospectively source-entailment gold.** It differs from
the historical event-relation gold for J1/J2. This must be explicit in preparation
and approval; it is not a repair of the TH/EC results or a newly counted historical
recovery. Exact old requests serve as repetition controls, and fresh matched
predictions test transfer. The new experiment measures claim-form discrimination,
not a general increase in model intelligence.

## Proposed scoring and interpretation

Freeze exact claim objects and expected labels before inference. Score each arm
against its own proposition; do not call a differing correct label a loss or
translate an unsupported source-entailment verdict into a false event verdict.

A useful discrimination signal should require:

- All **24 reports valid**, with invalid attempts retained in denominators.
- All **eight unknown-timing matched pairs** discriminate correctly: grounded,
  complete ER uncertain and SE unsupported across four families and both orders.
- All **eight individual same/different-time control reports** have the correct
  grounded target judgment, preserving the positive and negative relations.
- All **24 separate conditional components** remain usable, and no protected
  paired loss in non-target components, supported assignment/craft labels, or
  accurate complete explanations/citations. Check each whole-claim aggregate
  against its own claim form; do not require equal whole-claim labels across forms.

These are small diagnostic screens, not production qualification. Compare primary
labels and source-adjudicated behavior separately. Exact reference-list matching
is not the criterion; complete, accurate alternative witnesses are acceptable.
Freeze witness feasibility within the existing **eight-reference whole-claim union
and 2,000 summed reason-character** limits before approval.

Interpret possible results prospectively:

- Correct ER/SE divergence on unknown cases, with preserved controls, supports
  claim-form sensitivity and the evaluation-design explanation. It does not
  retroactively change TH/EC scores or establish better writing.
- Unsupported ER accompanied by accurate unknown-limit reasons leaves a label
  policy/claim-reading problem; it is not automatically invented opposite timing.
- ER accepts an unestablished actual relation, or a reason invents timing: retain
  that as a separate semantic failure.
- Invalid reports or incomplete execution leave the affected semantic comparison
  unobserved; report delivery limits without substituting repaired output.

Mask final assessments for source inspection and seal notes before decoding the
key. **Claim form will be visible in the claim text** and cannot be fully blinded;
hide arm/order/trial metadata, thinking and resources and disclose this limitation.
Capture latency and provider input/generated/cache counts, available timing/splits,
and resource use per usable report. Missing thinking-token breakdowns remain unknown.
