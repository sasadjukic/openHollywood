## Frozen scoring and interpretation

Validity, primitive correctness, projected correctness, explanation grounding,
citation adequacy, source coverage, conditional discrimination and efficiency
remain separate. All attempts retain their denominators. A correct projection
with a false or unsupported premise cannot earn usable-target credit.

The useful-signal screen requires:

- **40/40 valid deliveries**, without repair or replacement.
- **20/20 usable PO targets**: all **60** premise statuses correct and grounded,
  adequate evidence/reasons, reviewed visible scope, and both derived labels
  correct. Every fresh class must pass in both orders.
- All **four historical M5 targets** usable across BS/PO and both orders.
- All **40 conditional judgments** usable against their independent positive or
  negative gold; all **200 assignment/craft component judgments** usable across
  both arms. This includes the real assignment violations and qualified
  interpretations; it is not a demand for a preferred prose style.
- No protected BS-to-PO loss on delivery, a usable target/non-target or grounded
  whole/component explanation. These losses are reported even when another score
  improves.
- Recovery of **every contemporaneous valid BS unusable target**. Invalid BS
  deliveries do not create semantic recovery opportunities. Separate label
  recovery from grounding recovery. If no BS target failure recurs and all other
  screens pass, the result is **alignment without reproduced recovery**, not a
  comparative recovery claim.

Incomplete execution receives no advancement. Delivery, semantic or protected-
loss failures retain limited evidence without advancement. A successful comparison
can retain a source-provenance generalization signal only; it cannot qualify
production, authorize a writer edit or establish improved story quality.

The prior sixteen ER targets remain offline regression anchors. PO's event
projections are checked against independently authored event gold, but there is
**no new ER model measurement** in this comparison. OR/RO repetitions are not
independent stories, and family-specific failures cannot by themselves establish
a causal explanation.
