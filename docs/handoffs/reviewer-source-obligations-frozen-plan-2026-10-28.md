## PO contract and scope boundaries

For C02.A01 only, the model returns these three IDs in this order:

1. `same_entity`: the specified observations concern one physical entity.
2. `opposite_literal_descriptions`: their predicates could not both hold of one
   entity at one instant, considered independently of actual identity and time.
3. `same_observation_instant`: the observations refer to one instant.

Each requires a status, concise reason and evidence references. Allowed statuses
are `established`, `refuted`, `unestablished`, `not_reviewed` and `conflicted`.
Established/refuted require evidence; other statuses may use an empty reference
array with an adequate reason. The model must not return composite verdicts,
projection fields, scope-completeness flags or additional fields for this target.
All other components retain their existing supported/unsupported/uncertain shape.

The host binds the packet, target claim, observation pair, obligation map and
source scope. It derives both labels while preserving the raw primitive response.
Those projections are explicitly **provisional**. A filled ledger supplies only
a structural claim of review completion; it cannot prove semantic review. Source
inspection must independently judge every status, reason, citation and coverage,
including the entire visible packet. No projection authorizes a writer.

Under a completed review of a complete source, established identity/opposition
and unestablished time yield uncertain event relation but unsupported source
sufficiency. Incomplete declared scope or unreviewed/conflicted premises cannot
be treated as a completed source-wide negative. Explicit different identity/time
and explicit common-time positive controls prevent an always-negative shortcut.

The local algebra deliberately accepts a false but well-formed supplied premise
and produces a wrong result in a regression fixture. This demonstrates its limit;
the experiment tests the model's primitive judgments as well as the projections.
It does not claim that arithmetic, hashes or schema validity prove narrative truth.
