## Distinctions an adapter must preserve

**Raw output and application audit are different records.** In three unmet
checks, raw `recommended_resolution` is null; the saved audit contains advice
rendered from the model's shared finding conditions. Status, assessment and
selected handles agree exactly. The full historical validator replay verifies
the advice transformation. Three raw `reject` verdicts also normalize to
`revise`. Display raw response, normalized result and saved native audit with
their actual origins; never describe the application-added wording as a direct
model quote or silently replace the raw record.

**Available locally does not mean shown to the critic.** Current prose was shown
through the complete evidence catalog. All six post-review packets also retain
the full original draft locally, while its rendered artifact view contains only
repair-source identity. Specific historical repair quotations remain visible in
`repair_acceptance_tests`; the rest of that old prose is recovered after review.
Compacted Blueprint and critique views likewise remain distinct from full local
source bodies. The audit retains both source and actual rendered-view hashes.

**Probe versions are not production database lineage.** The archive declares
synthetic unapproved context and content-derived diagnostic version UUIDs. It has
no persisted successful creator invocation or canonical critique version for
these reviews. Its standalone plan key is `scene_plan`, whereas the SQLite export
expects the production `scene_plan_<scene_id>` key. Do not fabricate identifiers,
rename keys or import synthetic rows to satisfy the production export contract.
The proper origin is `diagnostic_probe`, with absent production identities left
explicitly null.

**Literal comparison is not semantic fulfillment.** All **55/55** selected
excerpts differ from their assignment fields. That is expected when prose
dramatizes an assignment. It is not 55 defects, a reason to rewrite, or a failed
quality gate. Multiple excerpts remain separate; no aggregate equality or
original-to-revision semantic correspondence is inferred.
