## Question and arms

Can separately delivered material-component judgments, followed by
application-owned aggregation, improve usable whole-claim assessment without
rejecting defensible craft readings or inventing facts in explanations?

| Arm | Proposed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| CV | Unchanged existing fixed-claim system, output contract, schema and sampling settings |
| CA | Complete original claims plus a pre-audited component map; explicit keyed component judgments, references and short explanations; application derives each whole-claim label |

CA is a diagnostic interface package: supplied decomposition, component response
shape and deterministic aggregation. A two-arm comparison cannot isolate the
individual contribution of each. MC is not added as a third arm; its completed
test failed its advancement screen. CV remains the contemporary control.

For a faithfully decomposed conjunction, any unsupported component prevents a
supported whole claim; uncertainty propagates when no component is unsupported;
all supported components yield supported. Preserve all component results and
explanations. No model-written final verdict can override the application result.
Factual support, explicit requirements and literary defensibility remain distinct.
Do not demand that a defensible craft interpretation be the only possible reader
response, or infer that an unsupported allegation authorizes any edit.

This is still a model-dependent semantic assessment. Application arithmetic cannot
correct a falsely supported premise, detect omitted material assertions, or

production verifier, prompt change, writer call, extra model or correction pass.

## Component map and evidence policy before freezing

The application supplies stable component IDs, neutral proposition text and kind
(fact, constraint or interpretation). The complete original claim stays visible.
Audit all components before inference for coverage, equivalent scope, negation,
quantifiers, modality, attribution and original/revision ownership. Split
relational assertions explicitly, including an alleged contradiction or
prerequisite; checking only the two quoted facts would miss the relationship
being asserted. Avoid turning a false proposition into its correct replacement.
The prior short material-check annotations are audit aids, not approved live maps.

Withhold component gold, expected whole labels, source adjudication rationales,
selected gold citations and historical outcomes. The map's human authorship is a
real assistance to the candidate and must be reported. This would test assessment
of supplied components, not autonomous decomposition of arbitrary new critiques.

Preserve **eight distinct references per whole claim**, with CA counting their
union across components. Reuse across components is allowed; duplicates within a
component, unknown refs, and a union above eight are invalid. Definite component
judgments need nonempty evidence; uncertainty may use an empty list with a reason.
The **sum of component reasons is bounded by 2,000 characters per claim**. Full
source inspection evaluates representative evidence, not a new demand to cite
every incidental detail separately. There is no cap increase, automatic reference
selection, trimming, normalization or salvaged-claim credit.

This keeps the semantic comparison from also becoming a cardinality-relaxation
experiment. New shape overhead is still a delivery risk. Freeze the exact schema,
request diff and validation failures before asking for live approval.

## Proposed scoring and diagnostic screen

Report delivery validity; whole-claim labels; component-level labels and omissions;
entire-reason grounding; citation support; and usable whole claims separately.
For CA, a correct aggregate cannot hide an incorrect material component, an
unjustified dismissal of defensible craft, or an unsupported extra explanation.
CV is assessed under the same complete-claim and full-reason standard. There is
no new automatic test that recognizes semantic truth from response wording.

Freeze each component's expected interpretation, exact source support and all
whole-claim gold before calls. Secondary B/C03 scope alternatives remain explicit
and outside primary scoring. Preserve its historical-gold descriptive result,
without using that gold to force a stronger component reading.

Provisional screen for retaining CA as promising diagnostic evidence only:

1. **At least 15/16 valid responses**, including **3/4 fresh**, and no paired loss
   of valid CV delivery.
2. **At least four paired usable primary recoveries across two families**, with
   one unchanged claim recovered in both orders and at least one fresh recovery.
   Both deliveries must be valid; invalid-to-valid gains are reported separately.
3. **No paired loss of a usable primary CV claim**, no new false rejection or
   abstention on a supported control that CV labels correctly, and no loss of
   grounded primary reason/citation support where CV was grounded.

Assess recovery opportunity from valid CV failures independently of CA success.
If the required opportunity is absent, report insufficient opportunity and add
no calls. Independent delivery or protected losses still prevent advancement.
Invalid/dispatched terminal responses remain failed attempted opportunities;
unstarted cells are reported separately. No partial or incomplete run advances.
Report by family, fresh/inherited origin and source order, not only totals.

Use masked IDs for source adjudication, seal notes before decoding, and disclose
that the component schema will reveal the arm. This is assistant factual audit,
not blind human literary preference. A positive result would support another
bounded integration design, not production promotion or better-story claims.
