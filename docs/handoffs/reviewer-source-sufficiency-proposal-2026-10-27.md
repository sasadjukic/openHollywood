# Proposed SE/BS/ER source-sufficiency comparison

Date: 2026-10-27. **PROPOSAL ONLY — at most 36 calls. Not prepared, frozen or
approved for inference.**

The [local audit](reviewer-source-sufficiency-audit-2026-10-27.md) finds grounded
missing-time explanations paired with incorrect K2 SE labels. It also finds a
neighboring timing-claim difference between K2 and the successful families. Test
one factor first: whether making the bounded source itself the grammatical
subject improves source-sufficiency judgments under the existing EC contract.

## Hypothesis and intervention

**A direct bounded-source sufficiency assertion may reduce confusion with an
unknown event relation, without changing the evidence or the factual-label rules.**
This is a wording intervention within the source-entailment claim object, rather
than another event-versus-source comparison or a new output format.

| Arm | Role |
| --- | --- |
| SE | Repeat the family's existing source-entailment wording |
| BS | State directly that the complete supplied prose and source context are sufficient to establish the particular simultaneous observation relation |
| ER | Retain the corresponding event-relation claim as an unchanged anchor |

Illustrative K2 BS wording, to be finalized and sealed only during preparation:

> The complete supplied prose and source context are sufficient to establish that the two particular inventory observations concern the same brass hook, one occupied by a coat and one holding no object, at one and the same instant.

Only the target component proposition, its corresponding whole C02 text and
necessary identities/hashes differ within a matched set. Keep complete prose,
source context, C02.A02 conditional, C01 assignment, C03 craft/qualification,
evidence registry, output schema, EC instructions and sampling unchanged.
No answer label, timing conclusion, new system hint, scope card, repair step or
writer call is added. Claims under assessment are not source evidence.

BS explicitly names the full supplied evidence scope. Preparation must verify
that this wider wording does not introduce evidence unavailable to SE: context
must not supply an independent time that the named records omit. With that audit,
SE and BS have the same prospective gold. ER retains its different claim object.
The intervention tests this complete wording package, not the causal effect of a
single word, sentence length or inferred internal representation.

## Scoring and what a result could establish

Freeze all components, whole claims, source scope and gold before inference.
Fully inspect the finite packet before assigning negative source-sufficiency
gold. Unknown event truth must not become a negative event fact. Keep label
accuracy separate from source-grounded explanations and citation coverage.

Proposed useful-signal screen:

- All **36 deliveries valid**. Invalid attempts retain their denominators and
  supply no semantic recovery evidence.
- All **12 BS targets** and **12 ER targets** usable, including both orders of
  every known and fresh family.
- All **12 individual positive/negative control targets** usable across the three
  forms. This prevents an always-unsupported BS response from passing.
- At least one actual, grounded SE-to-BS recovery in **K2 and fresh L1 separately**;
  BS must pass both orders regardless. Count recovery only where a valid
  contemporary SE target fails and its BS counterpart passes. If those SE
  failures do not recur, report successful alignment without claiming recovery.
- All **36 conditional controls** usable and no protected loss from SE to BS or
  ER in non-target components, supported craft/assignment labels, complete reasons
  or citations. Score whole C02 against its own proposition and aggregation rule.

Preserve adequate alternative witnesses; do not demand exact withheld reference
lists. Verify the existing eight-reference whole-claim union and 2,000 summed
reason-character limits. Keep the new forbidden-field fixtures and qualified
temporal-wording fixtures as local checks, not extra live arms or parser repairs.

Possible outcomes must remain distinct:

- BS recovers known and fresh SE errors while ER and other controls remain sound:
  retain evidence for the bounded-source wording under these conditions.
- SE and BS both align: the failure did not reproduce; no causal recovery shown.
- BS still returns uncertain while accurately identifying missing evidence:
  the proposed wording is insufficient. Do not auto-correct its label.
- BS invents temporal facts, damages craft judgments or returns invalid output:
  report those separate failures and retain no advancement.

This comparison cannot isolate the effect of C03 timing cues, which stay fixed
within each family. If wording fails despite stable ER and delivery, a later
claim-neighborhood experiment could vary that cue alone. Do not combine that
second intervention with this one or launch it automatically.

Mask final outputs for source inspection; hide arm/order/trial metadata,
resources and thinking, and seal notes before decoding. Claim wording remains
visible, so disclose partial masking. Measure paired latency/generated-token
ratios and resources per usable report; retain unknown token splits and billed
cost as unknown. Both forms of source claim and ER use high thinking, so this
does not re-test thinking on versus off.
