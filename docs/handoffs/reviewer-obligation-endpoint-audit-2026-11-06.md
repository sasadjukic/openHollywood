# Obligation scope and endpoint evidence audit

Date: 2026-11-06. **Complete locally; no provider, input-count, catalog or daemon calls.**

The [ordinary-review transfer result](../benchmark_reports/reviewer-ordinary-transfer-v61-results-2026-11-06.md)
remains **87/96 grounded, eight citation-only failures and one semantic scope
failure**. This audit preserves the failed and recovered responses, successful
controls, complete context and original scoring. It does not publish corrected
model accuracy or qualify writer dispatch.

The main finding is that the assessment contract needs to distinguish **an
obligation, an outstanding repair, a forbidden event before a deadline and a state
at the endpoint**. These can concern the same story detail but require different
judgments and different evidence. A uniform requirement to cite the ending is not
a general solution.

## Obligation truth and the surrounding repair

The selected field, “Elena must decide to verify the handwriting,” is nested under
`assignment_repair_conditions`, linked to `assignment:outcome`. The surrounding
ordinary review marks the outcome unmet and says the decision process was bypassed.
The current assignment, however, explicitly requires the decision, and the draft
depicts purposeful journal retrieval and comparison. The source task expressly
allows intentional action to establish a decision without a literal declaration.

The first CP assessment recognizes all of these facts, but returns `unsupported`
after evaluating whether an additional repair is outstanding. Its identical
repeat returns `supported` for the obligation while rejecting the implication
that another decision must be added. Both PC responses make that separation too.
The frozen mismatch is therefore retained as a scope failure; its explanation's
story facts are not treated as hallucinations.

This exposes a task-boundary risk. The wire supplies the exact whole field and
full surrounding report, and gives generic instructions about obligations and
occurrence. It does **not** supply the specific `assignment_obligation` declaration
used by the offline gold. The tracked declaration component is deliberately local
and `model_input: false`; the production/model boundary has not already integrated
those declarations. Adding a local audit record therefore cannot fix the model's
interpretation by itself.

Four authored local contrasts independently vary **required / optional** and
**performed / not performed**. The obligation's truth is unchanged when only
performance changes. None of the four grants edit authority. A missing mandatory
event may justify investigating a repair, but neither a true requirement nor a
mechanically complete declaration determines the best edit or its creative cost.

For future assessment, bind an explicit task to the unchanged field:

- **Obligation:** does the current assignment impose this condition?
- **Fulfillment:** does the complete current draft establish it?
- **Repair necessity:** is a further change warranted, considering both facts and
  the surrounding recommendation? This remains a separate, unqualified judgment.

Preserve the original report container and its recommendation as context. Do not
rename a repair question into an obligation question and claim they are equivalent,
or interpret a supported obligation as an automatic instruction to rewrite.

## Endpoint evidence: a limitation in the frozen criterion

All four Elena responses cite the explicit match paragraph and the no-match
assignment for Q005/Q006, but omit the final paragraph. The frozen criteria require
ending context, so the **eight original citation-only failures remain**. Each
response had capacity for at least four more witnesses; missing context and resource
exhaustion do not explain the omission.

The audit finds that the rule needs more precise justification before reuse:

| Question | Evidence obligation |
| --- | --- |
| Did a forbidden event occur within a bounded interval? | Applicable authority plus an established occurrence within that interval can suffice to demonstrate a breach. A later return to the starting state does not erase the event. |
| Does a state hold at the endpoint? | Applicable authority and evidence establishing that endpoint state. An earlier state may be insufficient. |
| Was a state maintained throughout? | A single contrary event can refute compliance. Supporting compliance needs complete relevant coverage or an adequate explicit whole-interval statement. |
| Does the complete available scene omit an event? | Complete relevant scope or an explicit nonperformance statement; the last paragraph alone is not generally an absence proof. |
| Does the explanation add a material fact about the ending? | The cited evidence must cover that fact as well as the selected claim. |

These are distinctions for authored evaluation design, not an automatic semantic
rule engine. They assume the cited narrative establishes the relevant event or
state; beliefs, hypotheticals, unreliable statements and explicit later correction
must still be assessed in full context.

In the saved Elena source, the boundary policy explicitly distinguishes decisions,
attempts and established results, and says stronger later proof does not authorize
an earlier result. Q006 alleges overrun **by establishing the match**. Q005 compares
that establishment with the no-match deadline; its offline material inventory adds
“establishment persists through ending,” which is not separately explicit in the
selected sentence. The final paragraph preserves sender/origin uncertainty without
withdrawing the match.

Consequently, the eight omissions do not by themselves establish eight failures
to cite evidence logically necessary under every defensible reading of those
claims. The current gold commits to a stricter endpoint/persistence reading.
Before another experiment, identify that reading explicitly or use newly authored
claims that distinguish occurrence-by-deadline from endpoint state. Do not silently
relax the old criterion or retroactively treat a new interpretation as the old task.

There is also a narrower, concrete coverage concern: **two of the eight responses**
(Q005 in both CP runs) discuss the remaining sender/delivery uncertainty in their
reasons without citing its final paragraph. If that fact is material to the
explanation, it needs its own cited coverage. The other six omit the ending under
the frozen rule without making that same explicit qualification. This is a subset
analysis of the eight existing failures, not an added failure count or a new score.

The local fixtures include eight endpoint/evidence contrasts. They show that
closing a gate after opening it can satisfy an endpoint-state requirement while
still violating a no-opening deadline or throughout requirement. They distinguish
complete absence review from a last-paragraph shortcut and retain an explanation
that needs ending evidence independently of its core event claim. One deliberately
bad declaration names an irrelevant final paragraph as endpoint evidence: the
section-set checker reports mechanical coverage, while semantic suitability stays
unverified. This countercontrol prevents presenting the checker as a truth oracle.

authority, material components and unresolved qualifiers. It does not have a
dedicated temporal-quantifier field, infer a correct interpretation, or assess the
semantic adequacy of model citations. This audit uses its existing unresolved-
qualifier display; it does not extend the production contract.

Three **local draft work-brief previews** demonstrate an explicit obligation task
and possible establishment-by-deadline tasks alongside the same full work/context.
They contain no expected labels, chosen witness IDs, repair instructions or hidden
gold rationales. They are author-reviewed proposals, not frozen requests or a
validated automatic transformation. The two temporal previews choose a particular
reading and require new pre-response admission; they are not replacements for the
historical gold. Their shape validator rejects extra answer/repair fields and
changed selections, but cannot prove arbitrary free text is neutral or correct.

All native audit fields still leave factual support, adequacy, edit necessity and
craft unassessed; writer authorization and whole-relation credit remain false.
Historical U responses remain in their original schema, with no invented native
delivery receipts or appended citations.
