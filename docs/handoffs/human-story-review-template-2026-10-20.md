# Human story review template

Review the story as a reader. Focus on its overall effect rather than checking
counts, finding exact phrases, or reconstructing model tests. For this informal
review, a blank substantive comment means no major concerns once the reader
confirms they have finished. A blank score, date, or confidence field is not an
implied value.

The coordinator prepares mechanical evidence before reading begins and supplies it
after the first impression is recorded. Do not put prior model verdicts, writer
explanations, or expected preferences into the first-pass reading material.

## Coordinator preparation checklist

- Select one full version or one meaningful pair per story family for an informal
  review. Keep near-identical repair variants in machine diagnostics by default.
- Freeze the prose and identities; retain any A/B mapping separately.
- Prepare exact counts, diffs, completion checks, and recorded editing-request
  results without asking the reader to rediscover them.
- For semantic obligations, supply grounded evidence and uncertainty rather than
  claiming that a word match proves an intention, decision, or causal relationship.
- Show the appendix after the initial impression. Ask only about material conflicts
  or ambiguous cases that need a reader's judgment; do not require a second audit.
- Preserve blank substantive comments as no major concerns after reading is confirmed.
  Never invent scores, confidence, timestamps, or a changed preference.
- Keep human judgments, deterministic checks, historical model outputs, and new
  assistant interpretations separately attributed.

This short form supports informal scene/story diagnostics. A formal benchmark still
requires the accepted complete-story rubric, frozen coverage, comparable baseline,
and recorded human scores; do not derive those scores automatically from these
free-text answers. This is evaluation work, not a new production human checkpoint.
