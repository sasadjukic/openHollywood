## Frozen scoring and decisions

Source inspection uses masked final assessments, complete sources, exact claims,
component maps and registries. It excludes thinking text, arm/order/trial labels
and resource observations. Notes bind all source/assessment hashes, quote source
evidence and are sealed before the key is decoded. Masking is partial: source
families and explanation style can be recognizable. This is a factual/source
audit, not a blind human story-preference test.

For each target, source inspection records whether the explanation addresses the
observed relation, accurately identifies an unknown limit, substitutes only a
conditional, has another explanation, or remains unresolved. This annotation is
not inferred from keywords or the returned label. Usability requires correct
primary labels and accurate, complete reasons and citations addressing the exact
proposition. Unknown targets also require the accurate unknown-limit annotation.

**Contract alignment** requires a contemporary valid TH target labeled unsupported
with a grounded, complete unknown-limit explanation, paired with a usable EC
uncertain answer. Require at least **six opportunities and six recoveries across
all four F3/G5/J1/J2 families**, with both orders in at least two families. Historical
failures cannot supply missing opportunities. Such gains are label-contract
alignment, not reasoning recoveries. Preserve legacy unsupported-as-unestablished
as a permitted reading of TH; it does not by itself assert an invented fact.

**Observed-scope recovery** separately counts a contemporary valid TH acceptance
of an unestablished actual relation, or a source-inspected substitution of a
conditional for that relation, paired with a usable EC actual-instance answer.
A replicated signal requires recoveries in **at least two families including a
fresh one**, with no new converse scope error in the unknown-source pairs. If the
failure does not recur, report **not reproduced**; if replication is insufficient,
say so. Label-alignment gains cannot establish scope recovery. Neither conclusion
can be asserted from an incomplete run.

A useful contract candidate must also satisfy every preservation screen:

- All **16 EC deliveries** valid.
- All **eight EC uncertainty targets** F3/G5/J1/J2 usable.
- All **eight EC preservation targets** H/J3/J4/J5 usable.
- All **eight fresh conditional components** usable.
- No protected paired loss in valid delivery, usable target/whole claim/non-target
  component, supported craft/assignment label, or grounded complete explanation
  and citation coverage. Record overlapping losses as overlapping.

A partial run yields `incomplete_no_advancement`; failed screens yield
`retain_limited_evidence_no_advancement`; insufficient alignment opportunities
yield `insufficient_alignment_opportunity_no_advancement`. Passing all screens
permits only `retain_contract_alignment_diagnostic_signal_only`. The separate
scope conclusion never overrides preservation failures or authorizes deployment.

Report final labels and source-adjudicated behavior separately. Capture latency,
provider input/generated/cache counts, available timing/token splits, paired EC/TH
ratios, and time/tokens per completely usable report. Include failed attempts in
accounting and preserve unknown terminal usage separately. No automatic latency
or production qualification threshold is added.
