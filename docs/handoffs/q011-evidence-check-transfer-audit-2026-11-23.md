## Proposed bounded comparison

1. **Pair B and E on all eight cases with Sol at high reasoning.** Use the same complete authored context, claim, source authority, schema and settings within each pair. Carry forward the diagnostic E sentence about the observation, its instant and its bearing on the claim. Substitute only the appropriate physical proposition for Elias standing at the sill. Keep the original Q011 pairs as offline controls.
2. **Counterbalance order.** Use B first in one family and E first in the other for each ending type. Give each family two B-first and two E-first pairs. One generation per arm and case gives sixteen serial counts before up to sixteen generations, **32 endpoint calls maximum, zero retries**. Freeze exact source-bound requests, token, time and cost ceilings, evidence forms and single-use stops locally before seeking live approval.
3. **Score evidence and judgment independently.** Mask arm, label and order while checking both plan fields, the depicted first action and the final position in the cited draft. Seal passage-specific decisions before joining labels to gold. Report the four unresolved pairs individually, the four determinate controls, per-axis gains and losses, and baseline passes.
4. **Interpret the pairs by family and by failure type.** An incomplete comparison is inconclusive. Require E to pass both axes on all eight cases. To call a recovery replicated across families, require a B joint miss becoming an E joint pass on an unresolved case in **each** family. Report whether both failure types recovered and where. If B already passes every case, record no observed recovery opportunity. Gains confined to one family remain family-specific. A failed E control or unresolved case holds advancement.

This proposal tests whether the timing question transfers to a new character and to a moving object. The two short authored scenes isolate that distinction. A positive result would leave ordinary-review precision, writer behavior and story quality to be evaluated separately. B remains the default factual-assessment wording. Production prompts are unchanged, live inference is paused and the canary is on hold.
