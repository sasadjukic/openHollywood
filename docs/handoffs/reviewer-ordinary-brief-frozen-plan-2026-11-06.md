## Scoring and decision rules

Inspect **every fixed slot** against its unchanged whole field and material
qualifiers. Store raw delivery, label, explanation correctness, whole-claim scope,
witness binding, evidence adequacy and exact receipt/result hashes separately.

- **Unavailable:** no usable delivery; retains its place in the 128-slot denominator.
- **Semantic failure:** wrong label, a substituted question, an unaddressed material
  clause, or a materially false explanation. Correct component labels do not prove
  the whole relation. Log ancillary concessions separately.
- **Citation-only failure:** whole-claim semantics pass, but a witness is unresolved
  or the credited sections omit a material premise of the claim or explanation.
- **Grounded:** correct whole-claim assessment and materially correct explanation,
  with all witnesses bound and adequate evidence for all material premises.

Quotations are exact unique section locators: credit their complete registry field
or single prose paragraph, never uncited neighbors. No mandatory ending citation
when another section establishes the proposition; no endpoint-only shortcut for a
whole-scene claim. The retained limits allow up to eight distinct witnesses per
claim, 512 characters each, 8,000 quoted characters overall, 1,200 reason characters
per claim and 8,000 overall. Final output is capped at 64,000 characters. The
illustrative gold uses at most seven witnesses. Runtime requires exactly the four
selected field/quote identities; extra or missing assessments are terminal.

Report 64 matched U/B pairs, 64 repeat pairs and 64 order pairs, plus each unique
claim across its four slots per condition. **B must ground all 64 slots, recover
at least one paired U semantic/citation failure, and introduce no regression** to
pass the positive brief screen. All four B slots must pass for a claim-level gate.
Both arms fully grounded is a ceiling result, not evidence of B advantage. Stable
wrong answers fail. Missing slots, post-hoc gold repairs or changed interpretations
cannot rescue a gate. No semantic early stopping.

Success supports only further evaluation of a read-only authored task contract.
It does not qualify natural-error precision, automatic brief authoring, repair
necessity, writer dispatch, prose preservation or story quality. Feel, flow,
character and story-breaking details remain separate human review concerns.
