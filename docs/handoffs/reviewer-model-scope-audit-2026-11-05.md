## Production contract audit

The findings are tied to exact code excerpts in `production-contract-excerpts.json`.

1. **Active scope currently means identity and coverage.** The tracked
   [assessment work](../../engine/open_hollywood_engine/evaluations/assessment_work.py)
   and [result component](../../engine/open_hollywood_engine/evaluations/assessment_result.py)
   bind full context, selected statements, active unit IDs and evidence locations.
   They do not declare whether a selected natural-language statement asks about
   event occurrence, plan content, an assignment obligation or editorial effect.
   A single model-authored support label cannot resolve that meaning by itself.
   Their explicit unassessed authority is appropriate and remains unchanged.
2. **The production critic already separates compliance and craft.**
   `COMPLIANCE_CRAFT_RULE` preserves fulfilled obligations, permits actions and
   indirect narration as fulfillment, and keeps optional preferences from forcing
   revision. It also preserves genuine independent craft defects that can warrant
   revision even when assignments are met. `COMPARISON_RULE` asks what distinct
   craft defect remains after the minimal assignment repair, and requires mixed
   criticism to preserve the repeated part and distinct craft part separately.
3. **Application validation does not prove those comparisons true.**
   `comparison_error` checks structure and coverage of finding references.
   `_normalize_critic_craft_issues` preserves a correctly routed independent issue,
   including a blocking craft finding. The local authored control demonstrates that
   a structurally valid but unproved comparison can pass shape validation. This is
   an explicit semantic boundary, not evidence that a new parser could determine
   artistic necessity from the same fields.
4. **Current endpoint authority remains independent.** The boundary policy
   distinguishes a decision, an attempt and an established result. A more specific
   future proof does not license an earlier forbidden result. Thus rejecting H1's
   private-procedure allegation must not erase its genuine current endpoint breach.
   Conversely, that breach must not erase the earlier fulfilled decision.

For H1, whether correcting the current endpoint would also remove the pacing
concern is a useful comparison question. **No revision was produced or evaluated**
here, so the audit does not certify a redundant repair or an independent craft
defect. Shared evidence and different category names cannot settle that relation.
