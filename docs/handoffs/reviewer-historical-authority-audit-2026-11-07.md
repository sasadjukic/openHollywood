## Historical evidence: what has authority

The relevant production path already performs useful application-owned work.
The [critic evidence resolver](../../apps/api/open_hollywood_api/services/production_model_executor.py)
resolves selected version-specific current-draft handles into exact excerpts;
the normal craft path rejects model-supplied `evidence` quotations. Boundary
findings use that resolver too. The
[repair-record builder](../../apps/api/open_hollywood_api/services/production_revision_acceptance.py)
then copies the saved issue's evidence into `original_evidence`, carrying the
source critique version, target draft version and issue index. The copy operation
itself is not an independent quotation check.

The [probe export validator](../../engine/open_hollywood_engine/evaluations/probe_review_evidence.py)
separately checks each visible repair quotation against the recovered, hashed old
draft. The present audit replays the repair builder and verifies the actual saved
chains: **three post-review packets, five quotations, all unique exact matches**.
R1 contributes one crossing excerpt, R3 two date excerpts, R4 two undecided-choice
excerpts. Their source versions, body/field hashes, offsets, critique targets,
issue indices, older revision numbers and same-scene bindings are retained.

All three old draft bodies were available **locally after the original review**.
The original and later comparison models saw old source identities and the repair
quotations, but **not complete old prose or the local export's verification proof**.
No recovered old body was inserted into historical model context or credited as
a model citation. The contexts remain synthetic, unapproved diagnostic probes;
they are not durable, approved production reviews. The broader original revision
alignment remains `unresolved_not_selected`.

| Evidence object | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical critic `claim` | What that critic asserted, including quoted dates or choice wording within the field | That every assertion actually occurred, or that the alleged defect required repair |
| `original_evidence` plus authenticated source binding | The exact passage occurs in the specified historical draft | Whole-draft meaning, absence elsewhere, narrative truth of an attributed/conditional passage, or a justified edit |
| Complete old draft | Context needed for a broader historical story assertion, when actually supplied and eligible | Current assignment authority merely because it is older prose |
| `requested_change` | What repair was requested | That the approved assignment required it, or that it remains necessary now |
| Current assignment/boundary | The requirement for the current assessment scope | Automatic proof of an earlier version's obligation or current fulfillment |
| Current draft | Depicted current actions, dates and states in context | What an earlier critic alleged or what the older draft contained |

An exact excerpt is therefore stronger than a bare allegation, but is not a
universal event-truth certificate. Historical obligation versions must also be
bound before claiming a violation against an earlier assignment. A source's field
name is not a substitute for that chain.

### R1: a genuine admission ambiguity remains

The selected field says **the previous violation was corrected**. It does not
explicitly say merely that a previous critic made an allegation. The frozen gold
and brief instead emphasize the reported target and preserved excerpt. U asks for
historical event verification; B accepts the excerpt and explicitly limits its
answer to the recorded crossing. B's cited excerpt is authentic in this archive,
so it is not accepting a fabricated quotation. U's caution is also not evidence
that it misunderstood the current story facts.

The local finding is an **insufficiently explicit historical authority/admission
contract**, not proof of the model's internal cause. Keep the four frozen semantic
failures and recoveries, but do not use them to claim general model incapacity or
that an authored brief may weaken any whole claim into attribution. Future work
must explicitly select between assessing the reported allegation and assessing
the historical event/violation. Ambiguous whole fields stay pending scope review.

### R4: historical attribution is material

R4 explicitly names the original unresolved-choice claim. Its historical `claim`
field can establish that attribution; it need not additionally cite every duplicate
`original_evidence` field. U's current assignment and ending citations do not
establish the historical attribution. The generic `accept_when` field does not
contain it either. B's history + current assignment + purposeful ending citations
address the selected comparison. No completed journal retrieval, handwriting
comparison or match is required by the decision claim.

## R3: separate corrected dates from realization

Q003 says the earlier criticism alleged a same-day card, then states the current
card is dated October 14, 2034 against October 14, 2024, establishing the required
ten-year gap. Its frozen material-component list names history, dates and that
relation. The frozen evidence rule and rationale additionally require realization.
The short B brief asks for historical attribution and current date comparison;
it does not separately name realization.

**Local materiality decision:** for a future assessment of this unchanged whole
field, require historical attribution, both current dates, and actual authority
for the required date relation. Do not add a separate mental-event predicate.
This is an authored prospective decision, stored with the old gold hash in a
separate artifact; it does not replace historical gold or yield a revised score.

The distinction is substantive. A scene can show two dates exactly ten years apart
while the character has not noticed them. Conversely, a mistaken thought about
ten years cannot make two same-day dates ten years apart. Four authored local
contrasts retain correct dates with no depicted realization, explicit failure to
notice, same-day dates with a mistaken thought, and dates with explicit recognition.
These are semantic audit examples, not new model results or natural-error evidence.
Absence of a depicted realization is not automatically explicit nonrealization.

Realization **remains material to Q002's whole turning-point fulfillment and
Q004's achieved-state claim**. It also remains in the actual story assignment.
This decision does not weaken that assignment or propose removing realization
from the prose. It prevents the evaluation of a narrower statement from silently
becoming a complete-turn compliance test.

For Q003, this audit reads “required” against the current assignment. The repair
request alone proves only what was requested. If a future task instead asks whether
a requested repair was carried out, that different authority must be explicit.
An eligible equivalent assignment/premise source is acceptable; illustrative refs
are not a whitelist. Cross-claim borrowing and uncited adjacent fields remain
ineligible. The final B Q003's S314/S546/S577/S578 set matches the locally identified
propositions, but **its original citation failure and B's failed gate remain**.
Its Q002/Q004 citations of S579 also show that this response did not simply fail
to notice the realization everywhere.

## Read-only component assessment

The current `DeclaredSourceScope` vocabulary has assignment, draft, definition,
plan and availability. It does not represent historical critic assertions,
authenticated historical excerpts or requested repairs as distinct roles. The
work-brief contract can describe those distinctions in authored text, but
[ADR 0044](../adr/0044-read-only-assessment-work-brief-previews.md) explicitly says
structural consistency is not verified semantic interpretation.

Do not map a historical critic claim to current `draft` or `assignment` merely to
obtain `declared_consistent`. No such mapping or new production contract was added
here. The local proof records keep history, visibility, mechanical binding and
semantic authority separate. They also retain R1's unresolved whole-field reading.
No successful binding, supported label or completed assessment authorizes a writer.

## Recommended next steps

1. **Implement one bounded, read-only authority/materiality integration slice.**
   Extend the existing local assessment preview with explicit historical assertion,
   bound historical excerpt and requested-repair roles; display source version,
   field, offsets, hashes, verification status and what was visible to the model.
   Retain a separate authored material-obligation list. Keep unresolved readings
   pending; preserve whole fields and full context. A binding verifies copying,
   not entailment, historical violation or edit necessity. Do not add a workflow
   checkpoint or connect this preview to prompts, gates or writer dispatch.
2. **Admit both positive and negative authority controls locally.** Distinguish an
   authentic excerpt from an unverified assertion, historical attribution from
   event truth, requested repair from canonical requirement, and a date relation
   from a realization claim. Prefer saved ordinary failures with defensible whole
   gold; clearly label any authored negative controls. All-positive selections
   cannot demonstrate rejection of false criticism. Keep an unchanged known-case
   control and seek fresh scene families instead of repeating only these stories.
3. **Only if those checks justify inference, prepare a provisional 24-attempt
   B/H authority-receipt comparison:** three packets × two conditions × two
   presentation orders × two exact repeats, using Sol-high and a small equal
   workload. B retains the explicit work brief; H adds an application-rendered
   provenance/visibility receipt without answers or extra old prose. Both arms
   use the same prospectively audited claims, gold and material obligations.
   Hypothesis: explicit evidence authority reduces historical-scope uncertainty
   without promoting allegations into events or requests into requirements.
   Require false-claim/authority controls as well as recoveries, adequate evidence
   and repeat stability. Freeze exact payloads, resource ceilings and stops only
   after local admission; obtain approval before either story-bearing endpoint.
   **No next cases, requests, budget or live authorization are frozen here.**

This sequence addresses an application/evaluation ambiguity before spending more
inference. It does not establish automatic brief authorship, natural-error
precision, necessary editing, preservation or improved story quality. For future
craft work, human review remains about feel, flow, character and story-breaking
details; machines should supply mechanical evidence. A missing evaluation citation
or an overbroad rubric must not itself trigger another prose rewrite.
