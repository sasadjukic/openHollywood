## What qualifies, and why

The [readable admission record](../../data/diagnostics/ordinary-review-admission-2026-11-12/admission.md)
contains every admitted and held whole claim. The
[structured controls](../../data/diagnostics/ordinary-review-admission-2026-11-12/controls.json)
add raw-field pointers, request/final fingerprints, material components, evidence
locations, rationale and adequate-evidence requirements. Gold is the inspecting
assistant's prospective judgment, not independent human adjudication or an
automatically proven truth.

| Controls | Admitted distinction |
| --- | --- |
| P01–P06 | Purposeful action can establish a decision; dates plus realization are distinct requirements; touch and psychic pull need no invented causal order; an explicitly unresolved choice fails a decision requirement. |
| P07–P12 | Compound assignments and terminal state: earlier boots-outside evidence does not cancel later entry; decision and no-crossing are separate; a general handwriting match violates an explicit no-match obligation without proving the private-flourish procedure. |
| P13–P14 | Original allegation, authenticated earlier quotation and current correction are separate. A comparative removal claim needs earlier-event evidence as well as attribution. |
| U01–U02 | The current boundary violation is supported, but the asserted next-scene starting state is unavailable in the actual visible authority. The whole claim stays uncertain under that declared reading. |

For the two repair reviews, the original quotation occurs exactly once in the named
earlier draft and is absent from the complete revision. Critique identity, target,
issue description/evidence/recommendation and visible repair test agree. These are
two receipts for **one unique earlier quotation**, not two independent historical
examples. Exact copying verifies provenance; semantic support still requires reading.

For U01/U02, the visible next-scene block supplies blueprint/scene identity, turn
and outcome, but no entry state or subsequent scene prose. The complete visible
packet was inspected. A fuller blueprint recovered in the saved input packet does
not retroactively become evidence shown to that critic. Unavailable does not mean
false, and the supported current-boundary clause does not settle the whole claim.

## Why apparent negative claims remain held

The strongest candidate, N02/Q007, says a general handwriting match achieves the
next scene's private-flourish turning point. A literal claim that the named procedure
occurred is not established by the prose. But the same field can reasonably mean
the draft consumed that turn's dramatic work. Assigning factual-negative gold would
silently decide between those readings. H03 preserves the unchanged whole field
and both interpretations.

Other holds mix factual overrun with editorial loss, use temporal shorthand such as
“ends exactly as,” or place entry in a future unit without clear starting-state
authority. They were not shortened into easier claims. Their factual clauses can
be understandable while whole-field gold remains unsuitable for this diagnostic.

N10 contains the exact bracing sentence twice. That mechanical count is recorded.
The critic missed the repetition, but its praise of imagery is not an assertion
that no repetition exists. We retain that omission without inventing a contradicted
factual claim from stylistic praise. None of this overturns the user's assessment
of the samples' overall story potential. Feel, flow, character and story-breaking
effects remain separate from mechanical and factual checks.

## Recommended next step

Implement a **small tracked read-only ordinary-review admission/intake path**, using
these local prototypes and the existing assessment components. Start with the
already supported saved-review/probe export contracts; refuse unsupported archives
instead of treating this private recovery helper as a general production adapter.

The deliverable should accept explicit whole-field selections and authored scope/
brief declarations, retain original evidence and operator provenance separately,
and export pending assessment work plus explicit holds. No automatic claim extraction,
gold inference, completed assessment, provider dispatch or writer routing is needed.
Verify supported, unavailable and ambiguous fixtures; prove that holds remain
visible, provenance does not become authority and replay preserves full context.
Any later support for the additional archives needs its own registered contract.

Alongside that integration, record an explicit policy for interpreting a named
procedure versus a claim about its editorial effect. Use H03 and the other holds
as examples; preserve their original text and earlier scoring. Do not ask a model
to settle ambiguous gold and then use its answer as independent validation.
