## Whole-claim decisions

- **Supported** requires every material clause, including assignment authority,
  temporal scope and endpoint conditions. The supported ferry claim needs both
  boarding and return, plus the actual ending and assignment; its boarding locator
  alone is insufficient.
- **Contradicted** maps to the existing response label `unsupported` and requires
  actual opposing source evidence. F02 Q001 preserves the true lifting action while
  rejecting the alleged earlier prohibition. The later covering rule cannot be
  applied retrospectively to the earlier scene. F03 Q003's conditional quotation
  is authentic, but the complete draft explicitly withholds the claimed procedure
  completion and stamp use.
- **Unavailable** maps to `uncertain`. Each claim concerns the contents of a named
  absent draft/report/certificate. Those bodies have explicit availability records
  and no invented contents. Current scene facts or another version's assignment
  cannot establish what those missing documents actually say. Missing evidence is
  not relabeled as contradiction.

The unavailable claims are compatible with more than one possible missing-document
content: a later departure could differ in passengers/order; a conservation report
could reach either glaze-origin conclusion; a certificate could confirm or deny
common authorship. These are operator counterfactual checks, **not** supplied source
facts or fabricated missing documents.

F02's authentic placement quotation remains an evidence locator. It does not prove
intactness or non-removal by itself; Q003 needs the earlier review for attribution
and the complete older prose for the event facts. Historical full-body and quote
bindings are checked separately from adequacy. Equivalent complete evidence remains
permitted; examples are not a new general citation whitelist or a cap change.
