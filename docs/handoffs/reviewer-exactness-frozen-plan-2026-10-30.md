## Scoring frozen before observation

Delivery, semantic grounding, literal precision, explanation materiality and
resources remain separate. All reserved attempts retain their denominators.
Inspection covers all final reports and leaf reasons, including every premise;
aggregate reasons do not count the same precision assertion again.

- **Semantic view:** inspect the complete scoped proposition against full
  sources. Correct labels require independently supported reasons and adequate
  citations. False premises behind a correct projected label fail. A receipt
  cannot supply semantic truth.
- **Strict explanation view:** preserve all false and ambiguous literal claims
  in the reason/citation annotations. Never silently remove them from the report.
- **Literal assertions:** bind each inspected assertion to exact reason offsets
  and text. Assertions about the four selected pairs are checked against their
  frozen receipts. Ambiguous referents remain unresolved. Assertions about
  other comparisons require explicit source inspection and are reported as such;
  the code does not automatically extract or disambiguate claims.
- **Materiality:** incidental false exactness can coexist with independently
  established semantic fulfillment. An error affecting the conclusion, evidence
  basis or repair advice fails semantic usability as a material review error.
  Relaxation from strict grounding requires a recorded precision issue; it
  cannot excuse an unsupported story premise. No annotation authorizes editing.

The useful-signal screen requires:

1. **32/32 valid deliveries** with no repair or replacement.
2. **112/112 EX components semantically usable**, including all **36 fresh EX
   discovery/decision/endpoint judgments**, **48 EX premises** and **16 source/
   event projection pairs**. Independent conditional and craft controls remain
   within those component requirements.
3. **No matched PO-to-EX loss** of valid delivery or usable semantic/source/craft
   judgment. Strict explanation losses are reported separately even when the
   semantic view passes.
4. **Zero EX definite literal errors**, and no increase in the total number of
   ambiguous-referent assertions over PO. Zero definite EX errors is the
   conservative operational screen chosen during preparation; existing EX
   errors cannot be hidden behind improvement elsewhere.
5. At least **one actual contemporary matched definite-precision recovery**:
   a valid PO component has a definite literal error; its valid EX counterpart
   has no false or ambiguous literal assertion and is semantically usable.
   Count recovery by family/order/component, not duplicated aggregate reasons.

With all other screens passing but no reproduced definite error, record
**alignment without reproduced precision recovery**. Incomplete execution earns
no advancement. Semantic, delivery, precision or protected-loss failures retain
limited evidence without advancement. Passing can retain a **receipt-precision
diagnostic signal only**, never production qualification or improved story quality.
Silence about copying is not itself proof of better semantic reasoning.
