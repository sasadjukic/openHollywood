containing registry entry was cited. Duplicate occurrences, missing text and spans

input are rejected. The result binds source and assessment hashes and never changes
the submitted list, infers semantic support or authorizes a writer.

Seven **operator-authored witness probes** were applied across the three matching
repeats: **21 checks**, with **14 located inside submitted citations and seven
outside**. The seven outside results span all six known citation failures; Q007
has separate date and realization witnesses. Five of those seven missing witnesses

selected from the canonical source because the reason paraphrases the event.

This is a mechanical demonstration on supplied anchors, **not a 6/6 automatic
detector or a model improvement**. The helper did not decide which words needed
checking. An exact phrase absent from the cited passage can expose a binding
conflict, but a legitimate paraphrase need not occur literally anywhere. Even a
perfect match may cover only one part of a compound claim. No whitelist of these
particular reference IDs is introduced.

## Private flourish: the relation being asserted matters

The source contract has three different propositions:

| Source | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S309, current draft `/prose` | Elena checks the journal and establishes a general handwriting match. | Performance of the specified private-flourish test. |
| S290, current assignment `/exit_state` | A match is forbidden by the current endpoint. | That every future handwriting-related event has already occurred. |
| S215, blueprint `/scene_plans/1/turning_point` | The next scene plans verification of a flourish only she knows. | An observed performance of that particular test in the current draft. |

The current overrun is supported by S290 plus S309. Under the frozen factual-premise
contract, treating the future private test as already fulfilled needs evidence of
that particular event, not just overlap in subject matter or purpose. Five reasons
fail that contract; the evidence does not identify an internal model mechanism
that caused the response.

There is also an evaluation-design limitation. **“Consumes the turning point” and
“preempts the later reveal” can express an editorial judgment about dramatic
function.** In one failed response, the model explicitly calls its conclusion a
structural inference from the overlap. Establishing identity early could plausibly
weaken a later reveal even if the private test has not been performed. That does
not establish the factual equivalence demanded by the frozen score, but the score
does not prove the editorial concern is worthless. We cannot determine from these
outputs alone whether the underlying problem is event conflation, a different
reading of the selected claim, or both. Future semantic testing must distinguish
“the specific test already occurred” from “the earlier match may weaken suspense.”
No historical judgment is silently relabeled to resolve that ambiguity.

Six authored local contrasts retain the distinction: general matching alone;
explicit performance of the private test; equivalent performance through paraphrase;
an explicitly unperformed private check despite a general match; an imagined future
check; and another character's different private check. The explicit and paraphrased
performance cases are positive controls. The other cases do not establish the
selected specific event. A lack of evidence is not a claim that an unstated event
is metaphysically impossible. These are authored expectations, not additional model
results or an implemented semantic classifier. Their selected factual claim says
that the specific test was performed; it deliberately avoids the ambiguous
editorial word “consumes.” A craft concern about diminished suspense must remain
available for literary assessment rather than being automatically rejected by
this factual test.

The story-quality consequence is narrow: a valid current boundary violation does
not justify demanding a rewrite on the additional unsupported ground that a later
revelation was consumed. Whether a general match weakens suspense can still be a
craft judgment, but it must not be presented as proof that the particular planned
test occurred. Grounded criticism also does not settle edit necessity or scope.

## Plan reference type: the object of the claim is decisive

H2/CP R3 correctly labels the already-inside allegation unsupported but wrongly
requires an observed next scene. The selected field is a **next-scene comparison**
about planned timing. Its source may establish what the plan says without showing
that a future draft enacted it.

The relevant blueprint fields are `/scene_plans/1/entry_state` (S221),
`/scene_plans/1/location_id` (S225) and `/scene_plans/1/summary` (S228), all carried
as `story_context`. The first describes caution/darkness, the second an interior
location and the third entry during the scene. None establishes a start already
inside. That is the appropriate reason for rejecting the allegation. The correct
current prohibition in S307 and the observed current crossing in S340 remain true
and independently establish a current violation.

Artifact role alone is insufficient: `story_context` contains planned scene
content, while `/prose` contains narrated events. The claim's referent, relevant
field, actor and time must remain explicit. An instruction to distrust plans as
observations must not become a rule that plans cannot support claims about plans.

Six authored controls distinguish an explicit already-inside plan; a plan whose
summary places entry during the scene; interior location without starting-state
proof; an explicit outside opening; an unavailable plan; and an observed next
draft under an observed-draft claim scope. An explicit planned start can support
the plan claim without a future draft. Missing sources remain uncertain. Explicit
counterevidence is distinguished from a merely unestablished relation.

The historical labels and explanations are preserved. The local controls guard
the evaluation contract; they do not automatically assign reference type to
arbitrary future prose or demonstrate that a prompt change fixes the model.
