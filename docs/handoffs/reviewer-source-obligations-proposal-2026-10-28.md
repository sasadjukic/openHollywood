# Proposed BS/PO/ER source-obligation comparison

Date: **2026-10-28**. **PROPOSAL ONLY — at most 48 calls. Not prepared, frozen or
approved for inference.**

The [local K2/L1 audit](reviewer-source-obligations-audit-2026-10-28.md) finds that
all four K2 source-claim failures already identify the missing common-time
evidence. Direct bounded-source wording recovers L1 but not K2. Another wording
reminder is not justified by this result. Instead, test whether independently
assessed premises can support reliable, application-derived judgments about the
event relation and the sufficiency of its source.

## Hypothesis and distinct measurement

**The model may supply accurate primitive source obligations even when its
composed source-sufficiency label is wrong.** Correct application projection could
then avoid that particular composition error without inventing a time or forcing
an uncertain event relation to false.

| Arm | Proposed task | Attempts |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| BS | Unchanged bounded-source sufficiency assessment | 16 |
| PO — premise obligations | Assess identity, literal opposition and same-instant timing separately for the same BS target; application derives both typed labels | 16 |
| ER | Unchanged corresponding event-relation assessment | 16 |

PO changes only the target assessment protocol. For C02.A01, supply three neutral,
case-bound obligations: same entity; literal descriptions incompatible if
simultaneous; same observation instant. Obtain a status, short reason and evidence
references for each. Candidate statuses are established, refuted, unestablished,
not reviewed and conflicted. Keep all other claims/components, the conditional,
full original/revision/context, source registry and original artifact identities.
No expected status, answer witness, source conclusion or gold label is supplied.

The application computes two outputs from these inputs and explicit scope:
event relation and source sufficiency. Neither is an extra model observation.
Retain the raw ledger; never convert the old BS/ER explanations into repaired
labels. Only inspection-validated premises may count toward usable recovery.
Scope completeness is application-owned. A completed response, populated field,
hash or list of references does not establish that the model reviewed the source
correctly; provisional projections remain subject to source inspection.

The existing local algebra is the starting point, with complete-source negative
conclusions, unresolved/conflicted cases and exact source/claim/pair bindings
tested. It is not a general natural-language entailment solver. The hypothesis
fails if the candidate merely shifts errors into its primitive statuses.

Prior premise-check instructions, whole-claim component aggregation and composite
evidence-status labels remain relevant controls from the investigation. The new
measurement is the three independently returned obligations **inside the relation
target**, followed by two explicitly different projections. It is a diagnostic
interface package, not an isolated word change or evidence that aggregation alone
improves semantics. It adds output complexity and may worsen delivery or latency.

The incomplete-record target must explicitly concern **S**, the whole declared
record, rather than **V**, the complete visible subset. Otherwise a negative
source-sufficiency answer could be correct and the planned uncertainty gold would
be invalid. BS and PO must ask about the same S. This is an explicit scope variant
in a fresh case; it is not a byte-identical historical control. Do not reveal the
appendix's contents or infer them. Validate the complete/unavailable boundary
before freezing. The context-time control prevents excluding authoritative context
merely because the prose entries have no timestamp.

Retain a real assignment violation, an independently supported conditional and
defensible craft/qualification claims in each family. Fresh referent claims must
match their actual case, including the distinct-object control. No neighboring
claim is evidence for another claim. No gold literary winner is supplied.

Preserve all **eight historical BS/ER wire requests** for K2/L1 × both orders
byte-for-byte under a matching baseline. For fresh BS/ER cases, retain the same
instruction, schema, sampling and claim-construction conventions, documenting the
explicit incomplete-S scope exception. PO keeps each matched packet and non-target
claims fixed; only its target protocol/schema and necessary identities differ.
Do not combine this with model, thinking, temperature, citation-budget or writer
changes. Family/predicate effects remain descriptive, not independently estimated.

Keep three-arm sets adjacent. Counterbalance their six possible arm orders across
16 sets as evenly as possible (two or three occurrences each), and balance source

## Prospective scoring

At preparation, author and inspect the full finite packets, primitive gold,
source scopes, claim objects, valid alternative witnesses and whole-claim
composition before freezing. Preserve current source-gold definitions and all
historical scores. An incomplete record is not an invitation to assume hidden facts.

Separate delivery validity, primitive accuracy, projected labels, explanation
grounding, citation adequacy and resource cost. The proposed useful-signal screen is:

- **48 valid deliveries**; invalid attempts retain their denominators and cannot
  create a recovery opportunity.
- **16/16 PO targets usable**: all **48 primitive judgments** correct and grounded,
  both projections correct, explanations complete and references adequate.
  A correct projected label from any wrong primitive receives no usable credit.
- **16/16 ER targets usable**, with the two event/source outputs distinguished
  correctly in every missing-time and incomplete-scope case.
- At least one valid contemporary **BS-to-PO recovery in K2 and in a fresh
  missing-time family separately**. PO must pass both orders of each family.
  Previously observed failure is not evidence that this run reproduced it.
- Preserve both L1 orders and all context-time, distinct-time, incomplete-record
  and distinct-object controls. These prevent always-negative source labels,
  automatic same-referent assumptions and fabricated missing times from passing.
- All **48 conditional controls** usable; no protected BS-to-PO loss in non-target
  assignment/craft labels, grounding, complete reasons or citations. Retain BS/ER
  control comparisons separately. Score whole C02 against its own claim object;
  do not mix source-sufficiency and event gold.

PO's three reasons and conditional must still fit the existing **2,000 summed
reason characters per claim** and **eight-reference whole-claim union**. Verify
feasibility locally before freezing. Reused references across primitives are fine;
unknown references, duplicates within an array, extra fields or missing statuses
remain invalid. Do not raise limits or repair captures mid-run. Alternative
adequate witnesses are accepted; absence cannot be established by a citation list
without review of the full declared scope.

Interpret outcomes explicitly:

- Accurate primitive judgments and matched known/fresh recoveries support the
  diagnostic projection hypothesis, subject to all controls and resource costs.
- K2 recovery without a fresh contemporary failure/recovery supports only a
  narrower known-case result plus fresh alignment; it does not pass the full screen.
- Correct BS and PO controls without reproduced failures show alignment, not recovery.
- Wrong primitives localize a remaining evidence-assessment failure. Correct
  primitives with wrong projection expose an implementation/contract failure.
- Invalid output, harmful non-target changes or unmet screens retain limited
  evidence without advancement. A terminal stop yields an incomplete experiment.

No outcome qualifies production behavior, writer edit selection or story quality.
Keep the two prior craft-wording concerns qualified; retain sensitivity analyses
without changing historical adjudication.

Inspect final outputs against sources with arm/order/trial metadata, thinking and
resource data hidden; seal annotations before decoding. The schema/claims reveal
some arm identity, so disclose partial masking and assistant factual-audit limits.
Report paired latency/generated-token differences and resources per usable report.
Do not label combined generated tokens as separate reasoning/final counts.
